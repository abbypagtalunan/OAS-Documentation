SECTION 1

Getting Started

This section explains what the Secretary does in MediLink, how to sign in, and how the Secretary screen is laid out.

In this section
  • 1.1 About the Secretary role
  • 1.2 Signing in
  • 1.3 The Secretary screen
  • 1.4 Account menu and signing out

1.1 About the Secretary role

MediLink, the Integrated Healthcare System of the Municipality of Oas, connects patients with the Municipal Health Office. The Secretary handles the front desk: patients, appointments, bills and follow-ups. As a Secretary you can:

  • view upcoming appointments, and reschedule or cancel them;
  • add new patients and update their profiles;
  • book an appointment for a patient with a doctor;
  • upload laboratory and imaging attachments to a patient's record;
  • add bills, correct bill amounts and record payments;
  • view, filter and print receipts; and
  • keep track of patients who need a follow-up visit.
IMPORTANT Sample data only. All patients, doctors, appointments, bills, receipts and contact details shown in this manual are sample data used for training. They are not real records.
NOTE Screens in this manual were captured from the live system on 24 October 2026 with a secretary account.

1.2 Signing in

  1. Open a web browser and go to medilink.oas.lguapp.com.
  2. On the MediLink home page, click LOGIN at the top right (Figure 1.1).
  3. Sign in with the secretary account given to you by the system administrator.
  4. After signing in, MediLink opens the Appointment page (Section 2).

1.3 The Secretary screen

PartWhat it does
1 Top barShows that you are signed in with the Secretary role.
2 Your profileYour photo, name and email.
3 Main menuAppointment (Section 2), Patient List (Section 3), Billing (Section 4), Reports (Section 5) and Follow Up (Section 6). The page you are on is shown in blue.
4 Account menuClick it for Account and Logout (1.4).
5 Work areaThe content of the page you opened. Most pages show a list on the left and the details of the selected item on the right.

1.4 Account menu and signing out

  • Click Account to change your profile, email or password (Section 7).
  • Click Logout to sign out. Always sign out when you leave a shared computer.
SECTION 2

Appointments

The Appointment page lists patients with booked appointments. From here you can see the details of an appointment, reschedule it or cancel it.

In this section
  • 2.1 The appointment list
  • 2.2 Viewing an appointment
  • 2.3 Rescheduling an appointment
  • 2.4 Cancelling an appointment

2.1 The appointment list

Click Appointment in the main menu. Each row is one appointment, shown by the patient's name. A patient with several appointments appears once for each.

To find a patient's appointments, type the name in Search patient. The list narrows as you type (Figure 2.2).

NOTE The search matches capital and small letters exactly. David or Cruz finds the patient, but david or cruz does not. Type names with a capital first letter.

2.2 Viewing an appointment

Click a patient in the list. The Appointment Details panel opens on the right.

FieldWhat it shows
Appointment DateThe date and time of the appointment.
Appointment ReasonsThe reason the patient gave when booking.
Appointment ServiceThe service booked, for example check-up.
Appointment FeeThe fee for the service, in pesos.

2.3 Rescheduling an appointment

  1. Open the appointment (2.2) and click RESCHEDULE APPOINMENT.
  2. In the Reschedule Appointment window, pick the new date under Date Picker.
  3. Pick the new time under Time Picker.
  4. Click SAVE, or CLOSE to keep the old schedule.
NOTE The message Date should not be before minimal date means the date shown is already past. Choose today or a later date.

2.4 Cancelling an appointment

To cancel, open the appointment (2.2) and click CANCEL APPOINTMENT.

WARNING Cancel only when the patient asks to cancel. This button was not clicked while preparing this manual, so check with the administrator whether MediLink asks you to confirm and whether a cancelled appointment can be restored.
SECTION 3

Patient List

The Patient List is where you add patients, update their profiles, book appointments for them and upload their laboratory and imaging files.

In this section
  • 3.1 Finding a patient
  • 3.2 Adding a new patient
  • 3.3 Patient details
  • 3.4 Booking an appointment for a patient
  • 3.5 Viewing and editing a profile
  • 3.6 Laboratory and imaging attachments

3.1 Finding a patient

Click Patient List in the main menu. All patients are listed on the left in alphabetical order. Type in Search patient to narrow the list, then click the patient's name to open the details (3.3).

3.2 Adding a new patient

When no patient is selected, the Add Patient form is shown on the right (Figure 3.1, box 3).

  1. Type the patient's First Name, Last Name and Middle Name.
  2. Choose the Gender, then enter the Birth Date and Birth Place.
  3. Type the address: Street, Barangay and City.
  4. Type the Occupation, choose the Civil Status (Single, Married or Divorced), and type the Religion.
  5. Type the Mobile number, and the Telephone and Email if the patient has them.
  6. Under Others, enter the patient's height (cm), weight (kg), pulse, temperature, blood pressure (systolic and diastolic) and choose the Blood Type.
  7. Click SAVE, or CLOSE to clear the form.
IMPORTANT Entering an Email creates a MediLink account for the patient with a default password (shown under the Email field). Ask the patient to sign in and change the password right away. The email becomes the patient's username and cannot be changed later.

3.3 Patient details

Click a patient's name. The Patient Details panel shows the patient's address, contact number, gender, birth date, birth place, religion and civil status. Below it are four tabs.

TabWhat it showsSee
AppointmentThe patient's appointments with schedule, reason, service, amount and status.3.4
ProfilesThe patient's full profile and health measurements.3.5
LaboratoryLaboratory files attached to the patient's record.3.6
ImagingImaging files attached to the patient's record.3.6

3.4 Booking an appointment for a patient

  1. Open the patient (3.3). On the Appointment tab, click APPOINTMENT.
  2. In Add Appointment, pick the date (Date Picker) and time (Time Picker).
  3. Type the Reason of Appointment.
  4. Choose the Doctor. Each doctor is listed with his or her specialty.
  5. Choose the Service. The services of the chosen doctor appear with their fees, for example check-up - ₱300 (Figure 3.4).
  6. Click SAVE, or CLOSE to cancel.
NOTE The Service list says No service added until you choose a doctor.

3.5 Viewing and editing a profile

The Profiles tab shows the patient's personal details and Health Records (height, weight, pulse, temperature, blood pressure and blood type). Fields marked * are required.

  1. Click EDIT.
  2. Change the details that need updating.
  3. Click SAVE, or CANCEL to discard your changes.
NOTE If the patient has an email, it serves as the patient's username and cannot be edited.

3.6 Laboratory and imaging attachments

The Laboratory and Imaging tabs list files attached to the patient's record, with the date and who added them (for example patient, secretary or imaging). Click the arrow (›) on a row to show the attached images.

To attach a file:

  1. On the Laboratory or Imaging tab, click ADD. The Upload images panel opens.
  2. Type a Description of the file.
  3. Drag the file onto the box, or click browse and choose it from your computer.
  4. Click UPLOAD.
NOTE When this manual was prepared, the attached images showed as broken image icons when a row was opened, and the upload panel says Select imaging raw result on the Laboratory tab too. Report these to the administrator.
SECTION 4

Billing

The Billing page lists patients with unpaid bills. Here you review charges, add bills, correct amounts and record payments.

In this section
  • 4.1 Unpaid bills
  • 4.2 Correcting a bill amount
  • 4.3 Adding a bill
  • 4.4 Recording a payment

4.1 Unpaid bills

Click Billing in the main menu. The Unpaid Bills list shows each patient with a balance. Type in Search patient to find a patient, then click the name.

The Unpaid Bill Details panel lists each charge (order) and amount, and the Total.

4.2 Correcting a bill amount

  1. Click the edit icon (Edit Amount) on the charge.
  2. Type the correct Amount.
  3. Type the Reason for edit.
  4. Click SAVE, or CLOSE to cancel.
IMPORTANT Always give a clear reason when changing an amount, for example a discount or a correction.

4.3 Adding a bill

  1. Click NEW BILL.
  2. Under Category, choose service to bill one of the clinic's services, or others for any other charge.
  3. For service: choose the Service; its fee is shown in the list, for example Vaccine - ₱700.
  4. For others: type what the charge is for (If others, please specify) and the Amount.
  5. Click SAVE, or CLOSE to cancel.

4.4 Recording a payment

  1. Click PAYMENT. The Total Cost of the unpaid bills is shown.
  2. Type the Amount the patient paid.
  3. Click SAVE, or CLOSE to cancel.
NOTE Receipts, with the payment and change, are listed in Reports (Section 5). Saving a payment was not tested while preparing this manual.
SECTION 5

Reports

The Reports page lists receipts so you can review collections and reprint a receipt.

In this section
  • 5.1 The receipts list
  • 5.2 Filtering by date
  • 5.3 Viewing and printing a receipt

5.1 The receipts list

Click Reports in the main menu. Each row shows the Date, Receipt No., Patient, Service and Rate. The Total of the listed receipts is shown at the bottom.

5.2 Filtering by date

  1. Click FILTER.
  2. Set Date & Time From and Date & Time To. Click the calendar icon to pick a date.
  3. Click FILTER in the window. The list shows only receipts in that period.

5.3 Viewing and printing a receipt

  1. Click the arrow (Details) at the end of a receipt row.
  2. The receipt opens with the clinic's name, address, contact number and hours, and the patient's name, address and date.
  3. Click PRINT to print it, or CLOSE to return to the list.
SECTION 6

Follow Up

The Follow Up page lists patients whom a doctor asked to come back, so you can contact them.

In this section
  • 6.1 Ongoing follow-ups
  • 6.2 Done follow-ups

6.1 Ongoing follow-ups

Click Follow Up in the main menu. The list shows the Date of the follow-up, the doctor's Description, the Patient and the patient's Contact Number.

  1. Call or message the patient using the contact number.
  2. When the follow-up is settled, click the thumbs-up icon (Set As Done) on the row.
NOTE Set As Done was not clicked while preparing this manual. Follow-ups already marked done are listed under Done (6.2).

6.2 Done follow-ups

Choose Done in the list at the top right to see follow-ups already marked done. Choose Ongoing to go back.

SECTION 7

Account Settings

Use Account settings to keep your profile, email and password up to date.

In this section
  • 7.1 Profile
  • 7.2 Changing your email
  • 7.3 Changing your password

7.1 Profile

Open Account from the account menu (1.4). The Settings page has three tabs: General, Email and Security.

  1. To change your photo, click UPLOAD PROFILE and choose an image.
  2. Edit your First Name, Last Name, Phone Number, State and Country. Fields marked * are required.
  3. Click SAVE CHANGES.

7.2 Changing your email

  1. Click the EMAIL tab.
  2. Type your New Email.
  3. Click CHANGE EMAIL.

7.3 Changing your password

  1. Click the SECURITY tab.
  2. Type the new Password, and type it again in Password Confirmation.
  3. Click CHANGE PASSWORD.
