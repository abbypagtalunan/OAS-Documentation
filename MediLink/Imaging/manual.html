SECTION 1

Getting Started

This section explains what the Imaging role does in MediLink, how to sign in, and how the Imaging screen is laid out.

In this section
  • 1.1 About the Imaging role
  • 1.2 Signing in
  • 1.3 The Imaging screen
  • 1.4 Account menu and signing out

1.1 About the Imaging role

MediLink, the Integrated Healthcare System of the Municipality of Oas, connects patients, doctors and the clinic's service units. The Imaging role is used by the imaging unit (for example ECG and X-ray). As Imaging staff you can:

  • find patients with imaging requests;
  • keep the list of imaging tests and their rates up to date; and
  • view the imaging transactions, open a result and print it.
IMPORTANT Sample data only. All names, tests, rates, results and contact details shown in this manual are sample data used for training. They are not real records.
NOTE Screens in this manual were captured from the live system on 24 October 2026 with an imaging account.

1.2 Signing in

  1. Open a web browser and go to medilink.oas.lguapp.com.
  2. On the MediLink home page, click LOGIN at the top right (Figure 1.1).
  3. Sign in with the imaging account given to you by the system administrator.
  4. MediLink opens the Patients page (Section 2).

1.3 The Imaging screen

3 Main menuPatients (Section 2), Test (Section 3) and Report (Section 4). The page you are on is shown in blue.
4 Account menuClick it for Account and Logout (1.4).

1.4 Account menu and signing out

  • Click Account to change your profile, email or password (Section 5).
  • Click Logout to sign out. Always sign out when you leave a shared computer.

2.1 The patient list

Click Patients in the main menu. Type a patient's name in Search patient to find the patient in the Patient List.

Doctors request imaging tests from the patient record (Imaging › UNSAVED › ADD TEST › CONFIRM ALL in the Doctor manual); no such request was pending, so the steps for opening a patient and entering a result could not be captured.
SECTION 3

Imaging Tests

The Test page lists the imaging tests the unit offers and their rates. Doctors choose from this list when they request a test.

In this section
  • 3.1 The test list
  • 3.2 Adding a test
  • 3.3 Changing a test or rate

3.1 The test list

Click Test in the main menu. Each row shows the Test name and its Rate in pesos, for example ECG at 120 and X-ray at 220.

3.2 Adding a test

  1. Click New Test. The Add Imaging Test panel opens on the right.
  2. Type the Test name.
  3. Type the Rate.
  4. Click CREATE, or the X to close without saving.

3.3 Changing a test or rate

  1. Click the edit icon on the test's row. The Update Imaging Test panel opens with the current name and rate.
  2. Change the Test name or the Rate.
  3. Click UPDATE.
IMPORTANT Doctors see the test name and rate when they request a test, so check new rates with the clinic before saving.
SECTION 4

Report

The Report page lists imaging transactions so you can review collections and reprint results.

In this section
  • 4.1 The transaction list
  • 4.2 Viewing and printing a result

4.1 The transaction list

Click Report in the main menu. Each row shows the Date, Transaction ID and Amount. The Total of all listed amounts is shown at the bottom.

4.2 Viewing and printing a result

  1. Click the printer icon on a transaction. The result opens, showing the test (for example ECG), the Result and the Impression.
  2. Click DETAILS to see the printable copy with the clinic's name and address.
  3. Click PRINT to print it.
  4. Click outside the window to close it.
SECTION 5

Account Settings

Use Account settings to keep your profile, email and password up to date.

In this section
  • 5.1 Profile
  • 5.2 Changing your email
  • 5.3 Changing your password

5.1 Profile

Open Account from the account menu (1.4). The Settings page has three tabs: General, Email and Security.

  1. To change your photo, click UPLOAD PROFILE and choose an image.
  2. Edit your First Name, Last Name, Phone Number, State and Country.
  3. Click SAVE CHANGES.

5.2 Changing your email

  1. Click the EMAIL tab.
  2. Type your New Email.
  3. Click CHANGE EMAIL.

5.3 Changing your password

  1. Click the SECURITY tab.
  2. Type the new Password, and type it again in Password Confirmation.
  3. Click CHANGE PASSWORD.
