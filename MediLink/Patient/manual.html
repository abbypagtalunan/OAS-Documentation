SECTION 1

Getting Started

This section explains what a patient can do in MediLink, how to sign in, and how the Patient screen is laid out.

In this section
  • 1.1 About MediLink for patients
  • 1.2 Signing in
  • 1.3 The Patient screen
  • 1.4 Account menu and signing out

1.1 About MediLink for patients

MediLink, the Integrated Healthcare System of the Municipality of Oas, keeps your health record in one place and connects you with the Municipal Health Office. As a Patient you can:

  • keep your personal profile and vital signs up to date, and check your BMI;
  • see the allergies, diagnoses, medication and diet advice your doctor recorded;
  • add your own diet plan and family health history;
  • view and upload your laboratory and imaging files;
  • follow your readings over time in the Health Graph;
  • find clinics, their schedules and their doctors; and
  • see which doctors have permission to view your record.
IMPORTANT Sample data only. All patients, doctors, clinics, results and contact details shown in this manual are sample data used for training. They are not real records.
NOTE Screens in this manual were captured from the live system on 24 October 2026 with a patient account. The patient screens did not show an option to book an appointment; appointments are booked at the clinic by the Secretary.

1.2 Signing in

Your account is created when the clinic registers you with your email address. Your email is your username.

  1. Open a web browser and go to medilink.oas.lguapp.com.
  2. On the MediLink home page, click LOGIN at the top right (Figure 1.1).
  3. Sign in with your email and password.
  4. MediLink opens your Profile (Section 2).
IMPORTANT If the clinic gave you a default password, change it the first time you sign in (Section 7.3).

  • Click Logout to sign out. Always sign out on a shared computer or phone.
SECTION 2

Profile and Vitals

Keep your personal details and vital signs up to date so your doctor sees correct information.

In this section
  • 2.1 Your profile
  • 2.2 Vital signs
  • 2.3 BMI calculator

2.1 Your profile

Click Profile in the main menu. Your profile shows your name, gender, birthday, birthplace, address, civil status, occupation, religion, mobile and telephone numbers, and under Other Information your height, weight and blood type.

  1. Click UPDATE. The fields can now be changed.
  2. Correct the details that need updating. Fields marked * are required.
  3. Click SAVE, or NO to discard your changes.

2.2 Vital signs

Click Vitals. The page shows your latest temperature (℃), blood pressure (systolic and diastolic, mmHg), heart pulse, weight (kg), height (cm), glucose, uric acid, respiratory rate, cholesterol and hepatitis status.

  1. Click UPDATE.
  2. Type the readings you measured. For Hepatitis, choose Positive or Negative.
  3. Click SAVE, or CANCEL to discard your changes.

2.3 BMI calculator

The BMI Calculator on the Vitals page uses your weight and height to work out your Body Mass Index.

  1. Check or change your Weight (kg) and Height (cm).
  2. Click CALCULATE. Your BMI appears under Result, for example 22.04 for 60 kg and 165 cm.
  3. Compare the result with the BMI Assessment table: below 18.5 underweight, 18.5 – 25 healthy, 25 – 30 overweight, 30 – 40 obese, 40+ morbidly obese.
SECTION 3

Records from Your Doctor

These pages show what your doctor recorded, and let you add your own diet plan and family history.

In this section
  • 3.1 Allergies
  • 3.2 Diagnosis
  • 3.3 Medication
  • 3.4 Diet
  • 3.5 Family History

3.1 Allergies

Click Allergies to see your known allergies, for example Penicillin. Only your doctor can add allergies. Tell your doctor if an allergy is missing.

3.2 Diagnosis

Click Diagnosis to see your doctor's diagnoses with their dates. Click the arrow on a row to show or hide the full diagnosis. Only your doctor can add a diagnosis.

3.3 Medication

Click Medication to see the medicines your doctor listed for you. Use the arrows at the bottom to page through a long list.

3.4 Diet

Click Diet. Suggested Diet on the left shows the diet advice from your doctor. Personal Diet on the right is your own plan.

  1. Click ADD under Personal Diet.
  2. Choose the Meals: Breakfast, Lunch or Dinner.
  3. Type what you eat under Description (required).
  4. Click SAVE, or CLOSE to cancel.

3.5 Family History

Click Family History to record the health of your family. This helps your doctor spot conditions that run in families.

  1. Click ADD.
  2. Choose the Category: Father, Mother or Children.
  3. Type the family member's Full name, Address, Birthday and Occupation.
  4. Describe his or her Health Condition, and choose the Current Status: Alive or Deceased.
  5. Click SAVE, or CLOSE to cancel.
SECTION 4

Laboratory and Imaging

The Laboratory and Imaging pages hold your test result files. You, the clinic and the laboratory or imaging staff can add files.

In this section
  • 4.1 Viewing your files
  • 4.2 Uploading a file

4.1 Viewing your files

Click Laboratory or Imaging. Each row shows the description, the date and who added it (for example patient, secretary or imaging). Click the arrow on a row to show or hide its images.

4.2 Uploading a file

  1. Click ADD. The Upload images panel opens.
  2. Type a Description, for example the test name and date.
  3. Drag your file onto the box, or click browse and choose it.
  4. Click UPLOAD.
NOTE When this manual was prepared, opened rows showed broken image icons, and the panel says Select imaging raw result on the Laboratory page too.
SECTION 5

Health Graph

The Health Graph turns your readings into charts so you and your doctor can see how they change over time.

In this section
  • 5.1 Choosing a reading
  • 5.2 Reading a chart

5.1 Choosing a reading

Click Health Graph. Each card explains one reading: Pain, Respiratory, Uric Acid, HDL, Potassium, Temperature, Weight, Calcium, LDL, Protein, Blood Pressure, Glucose, Chloride, Lithium, Sodium, Pulse, Cholesterol, Creatinine and Magnesium. Click DETAILS on a card to open its chart.

5.2 Reading a chart

The chart plots your recorded values over time. No record under the chart means no values have been saved for that reading yet. Use your browser's Back button to return to the cards.

SECTION 6

Clinics and Permissions

Find a clinic and its doctors, and see which doctors can view your record.

In this section
  • 6.1 Clinic List
  • 6.2 Permissions

6.1 Clinic List

Click Clinic List. Type in Search clinic to find a clinic, then click its name.

  • Other Info shows the days and hours the clinic is open and its contact number.
  • Location Info shows the clinic on a map. Use + and − to zoom. GET DIRECTION is for directions to the clinic (not tested for this manual).
  • Doctor List shows the doctors at the clinic and their specialty.
  • Click X to close the clinic information.

6.2 Permissions

Click Permissions. The table lists doctors with the Date and Status of their permission to view your record. Type in Search doctor to find a doctor.

NOTE No permissions were listed for the sample patient, so the actions on this page could not be shown.
SECTION 7

Account Settings

Use Account settings to keep your profile photo, email and password up to date.

In this section
  • 7.1 Profile
  • 7.2 Changing your email
  • 7.3 Changing your password

7.1 Profile

Open Account from the account menu (1.4). The Settings page has three tabs: General, Email and Security.

  1. To change your photo, click UPLOAD PROFILE and choose an image.
  2. Edit your First Name, Last Name, Phone Number, State and Country.
  3. Click SAVE CHANGES.

7.2 Changing your email

  1. Click the EMAIL tab.
  2. Type your New Email.
  3. Click CHANGE EMAIL.

7.3 Changing your password

  1. Click the SECURITY tab.
  2. Type the new Password, and type it again in Password Confirmation.
  3. Click CHANGE PASSWORD.
IMPORTANT Use a password that others cannot guess, and never share it.
