SECTION 1

Getting Started

This section explains what MediLink is, how a doctor signs in, and how the Doctor screen is laid out.

In this section
  • 1.1 About MediLink
  • 1.2 Signing in
  • 1.3 The Doctor screen
  • 1.4 Account menu and signing out

1.1 About MediLink

MediLink, the Integrated Healthcare System of the Municipality of Oas, connects patients with the Municipal Health Office. Residents book appointments online; doctors use MediLink to see their schedule, open each patient's record, and write findings, orders and prescriptions in one place.

As a Doctor you can:

  • see your appointments in a calendar (month, week, day or agenda list);
  • find a patient and open the patient's full record;
  • record vital signs, medication, allergies, diagnosis, diet advice, pain areas, notes and treatment plans;
  • write prescriptions, and request laboratory and imaging tests;
  • view laboratory and imaging results and attachments;
  • mark an appointment as complete;
  • manage the list of services you offer and their fees; and
  • view a report of your consultations.
IMPORTANT Sample data only. All patients, doctors, appointments, results, prices and pharmacies shown in this manual are sample data used for training. They are not real records.
NOTE Screens in this manual were captured from the live system on 24 October 2026 with a doctor account.

1.2 Signing in

  1. Open a web browser and go to medilink.oas.lguapp.com.
  2. The MediLink home page opens. Click LOGIN at the top right (Figure 1.1).
  3. Sign in with the doctor account given to you by the system administrator.
  4. After signing in, MediLink opens the Appointment calendar (see Section 2).
IMPORTANT Keep your password private. Each doctor must sign in with his or her own account so that every record shows who entered it.

1.3 The Doctor screen

Every page for the doctor has the same frame (Figure 1.2).

PartWhat it does
1 Top barShows that you are signed in with the Doctor role.
2 Your profileYour photo, name and email. Click your photo or name to open your account settings (Section 9).
3 Main menuAppointment (Section 2), Patient List (Section 3), Services (Section 7) and Reports (Section 8). The page you are on is shown in blue.
4 Account menuYour name at the top right. Click it for Account and Logout (1.4).
5 Work areaThe content of the page you opened.

1.4 Account menu and signing out

  • Click Account to change your profile, email or password (Section 9).
  • Click Logout to sign out. Always sign out when you leave a shared computer.
SECTION 2

Appointments

The Appointment page is a calendar of the consultations booked with you. From an appointment you can open the patient's record.

In this section
  • 2.1 The appointment calendar
  • 2.2 Week, Day and Agenda views
  • 2.3 Viewing an appointment

2.1 The appointment calendar

Click Appointment in the main menu. The calendar opens on the current month. Each appointment appears on its date as a blue bar with the time, the service and the patient's name.

  • Click PREV or NEXT to move to the previous or next month (or week or day, depending on the view).
  • Click TODAY to return to the current date. Today is shaded in the calendar.
  • Click an appointment to see its details (2.3).

2.2 Week, Day and Agenda views

Use the four buttons at the top right of the calendar to change the view.

ButtonViewUse it to
FirstMonthSee the whole month at a glance (Figure 2.1).
SecondWeekSee one week by hour, Sunday to Saturday (Figure 2.2).
ThirdDaySee one day by hour (Figure 2.3).
FourthAgendaSee a list of the month's appointments grouped by date, with the time of each (Figure 2.4).
NOTE In Week and Day views, scroll inside the calendar to see earlier or later hours.

2.3 Viewing an appointment

Click an appointment in any view. A Details panel opens on the right.

FieldWhat it shows
Firstname / LastnameThe patient who booked the appointment.
ServiceThe service booked, for example check-up.
ReasonThe reason the patient gave when booking.
StatusIncomplete until you mark the appointment complete (4.1).
  1. Click VIEW PATIENT PROFILE to open the patient's record (3.2).
  2. Or click X to close the panel.
SECTION 3

Patient List

The Patient List shows all of your patients. From here you open a patient's record.

3.1 Finding a patient

Click Patient List in the main menu.

  1. To find a patient quickly, type part of the patient's name in the Search box. The list narrows as you type (Figure 3.2).
  2. Click DETAILS on the patient's row to open the patient record.

4.1 Appointments tab and completing an appointment

The Appointments tab lists the patient's appointments with the date and time, service and status (Figure 3.3). When you finish a consultation, mark it complete:

  1. On the appointment row, click the arrow under Next Action.
  2. A Details panel shows the service, its amount (fee) and the reason for the visit.
  3. Click SET COMPLETE to mark the consultation as done.
WARNING Click SET COMPLETE only after the consultation is finished. This manual did not test what happens after the click, so check with the administrator whether a completed appointment can be reopened.

4.2 Information tab

The Information tab shows the patient's personal details: first name, last name, middle name, civil status, street, barangay, city, contact number and religion. You can view these details but not change them here.

4.3 Health Record and Pre-Lab

The Health Record tab has its own menu on the left: Pre-Lab, Medication, Allergies, Diagnosis, Diet, Pain Monitoring and Vitals Monitoring. It opens on Pre-Lab, which shows the patient's current health record.

The current health record shows temperature (℃), weight (kg), blood pressure systolic and diastolic (mmHg), glucose, heart rate, uric acid, respiratory rate and cholesterol. None means no value has been entered. To record new readings:

  1. Click UPDATE. The Update Health Records panel opens with the current values.
  2. Type the new readings in the fields you measured.
  3. Click UPDATE to save, or CLOSE to leave without saving.

4.4 Medication

Medication lists the medicines the patient should take and when.

  1. Click NEW MEDICATION.
  2. Under Meals, choose when the medicine is taken: Before or After Breakfast, Lunch or Dinner.
  3. Under Description, type the medicine and dose. Use the toolbar for bold, italic, underline, links and lists.
  4. Click ADD, or CLOSE to cancel.

4.5 Allergies

Allergies shows the patient's known allergies, for example Penicillin, in a text box with a formatting toolbar. Check this before prescribing.

NOTE No Save button was shown on the Allergies screen when this manual was prepared. Confirm with the administrator how changes to allergies are saved.

4.6 Diagnosis

Diagnosis lists the diagnoses recorded for the patient, for example Urinary Tract Infection.

  1. To add a diagnosis, click NEW DIAGNOSIS, type the Diagnosis and any Remarks, then click ADD.
  2. To read a diagnosis in full, click the view icon (2).
  3. To correct one, click the edit icon (3), change the text and click SAVE.
  4. The bin icon (4) deletes the diagnosis.
WARNING Deleting a diagnosis removes it from the patient's record. Delete only an entry made by mistake.

4.7 Diet

Diet holds the diet you suggest for the patient.

  1. Click NEW DIET.
  2. Under Meals, choose Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snacks.
  3. Type the advice under Description.
  4. Click ADD, or CLOSE to cancel.

4.8 Pain Monitoring

Pain Monitoring shows a body chart and a list of the pain the patient reported, with the date, description, pain level and whether the area is on the front or back of the body.

  1. Click ADD. The message Select part of the body which is in pain and rate it by saving it appears above the chart.
  2. Click BACK to show the back of the body, or FRONT to return to the front.
  3. Click the part of the body where the patient feels pain and rate it.
  4. Click SAVE, or CLOSE to cancel.
NOTE The rating step after clicking the body was not tested for this manual, so the screen that asks for the pain level is not shown.

4.9 Vitals Monitoring

Vitals Monitoring charts the patient's readings over time. Choose a reading at the top: BP, Temperature, Weight, Cholesterol, Glucose, Heart Pulse, Uric Acid or Respiratory. The chart title shows how many tests are recorded.

SECTION 5

Notes, Treatment Plan and Prescriptions

Use these tabs to write consultation notes, plan treatment and prescribe medicines.

In this section
  • 5.1 Notes
  • 5.2 Treatment Plan
  • 5.3 Prescriptions

5.1 Notes

The Notes tab lists your consultation notes by date with the initial diagnosis.

  1. Click NEW NOTES.
  2. Type the Initial Diagnosis.
  3. Type your findings under Notes.
  4. Click SAVE, or CLOSE to cancel.

5.2 Treatment Plan

The Treatment Plan tab lists planned treatments with their schedule.

  1. Click NEW TREATMENT PLAN.
  2. Check the Schedule Date. It starts with the current date and time; change it to the planned date.
  3. Type the plan under Treatment Plan.
  4. Click SAVE, or CLOSE to cancel.

5.3 Prescriptions

The Prescriptions tab has three parts on the left:

PartWhat it is for
LISTSaved prescriptions, with date and prescription number.
CLINICA prescription for a pharmacy chosen from the list (for example TGP or Malolos Pharmacy).
UNSAVEDA prescription you are still building. Add products, then save it.

To write a prescription:

  1. Click UNSAVED.
  2. Click ADD PRODUCT. Type the Product Name, choose the Type (Bottle, Box, Can, Capsule, Liniment, Sachet, Suspension, Powder or Tablet), type the Quantity and the Instruction (sig), then click ADD TO UNSAVE.
  3. Repeat for each medicine. Each one appears in the list with its quantity, type and sig.
  4. Click SAVE PRESCRIPTION. Saved prescriptions are listed under LIST.

For a pharmacy: click CLINIC, choose the pharmacy under Select Pharmacy, then add products and save the same way.

NOTE SAVE PRESCRIPTION stays grey until at least one product has been added.
SECTION 6

Laboratory and Imaging

The Laboratory and Imaging tabs work the same way: request tests, then view the results and attached files.

In this section
  • 6.1 Viewing results
  • 6.2 Requesting tests
  • 6.3 Attachments

6.1 Viewing results

Open the Laboratory or Imaging tab (use the › arrow on the tab row if needed). LIST shows each result with its date and ID.

  1. Click the document icon under Action on a row.
  2. The result opens. Laboratory results show each test (for example Hematology or CBC) with its description, result and normal values. Imaging results (for example ECG) show the result and impression.
  3. Click DETAILS to see the printable copy, then PRINT to print it.
  4. Click outside the window to close it.

6.2 Requesting tests

  1. In the Laboratory or Imaging tab, click UNSAVED.
  2. Click ADD TEST.
  3. Under Select Test, choose the test. Each test shows its rate, for example CBC - 120 or Hematology - 300 in Laboratory, and ECG - 120 or X-ray - 220 in Imaging.
  4. Click ADD TO UNSAVE. The test appears in the list with its rate.
  5. Add more tests if needed, then click CONFIRM ALL to confirm the requests.
NOTE CONFIRM ALL stays grey until at least one test has been added.

6.3 Attachments

ATTACHMENTS lists files attached to the patient's laboratory or imaging record, with the date and who added them (for example patient, imaging or secretary). Use the arrows at the bottom to page through the list and List to choose how many rows to show.

SECTION 7

Services

The Services page lists the services you offer to patients and their fees.

In this section
  • 7.1 Viewing and searching services
  • 7.2 Adding or changing a service

7.1 Viewing and searching services

Click Services in the main menu.

  • Type in Search service to find a service by name.
  • Click the view icon (3) to see a service's name and amount.

7.2 Adding or changing a service

  1. Click NEW SERVICE, or the edit icon (4) on a service.
  2. Type the Service name and the Amount (fee).
  3. Click SAVE (or UPDATE SERVICE when editing). Click CLOSE to cancel.
WARNING The bin icon (5) deletes the service. Delete only a service you no longer offer.
SECTION 8

Reports

The Reports page lists your consultations with the service and rate, so you can review your work.

In this section
  • 8.1 Viewing the report

8.1 Viewing the report

Click Reports in the main menu. Each row shows the Date, Patient, Service and Rate.

Type in Search report to filter the list. The search matches the patient's name or the service; for example, physical shows only physical examinations.

SECTION 9

Account Settings

Use Account settings to keep your profile, email and password up to date.

In this section
  • 9.1 Profile
  • 9.2 Changing your email
  • 9.3 Changing your password

9.1 Profile

Open Account from the account menu (1.4), or click your photo or name in the profile area. The Settings page has three tabs: General, Email and Security.

  1. To change your photo, click UPLOAD PROFILE and choose an image.
  2. Edit your First Name, Last Name, Phone Number, State and Country. Fields marked * are required.
  3. Click SAVE CHANGES.

9.2 Changing your email

  1. Click the EMAIL tab. Your current email is shown.
  2. Type your New Email.
  3. Click CHANGE EMAIL.

9.3 Changing your password

  1. Click the SECURITY tab.
  2. Type the new Password, and type it again in Password Confirmation.
  3. Click CHANGE PASSWORD.
IMPORTANT Use a password that others cannot guess, and change it right away if you think someone else knows it.
