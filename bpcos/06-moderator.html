Section 6

The Moderator

The Moderator handles the permits that the Building Official approved. After the permit is approved and the fees are paid, the Moderator records the Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC), uploads its certificate and prints the building permit.

Account. The role of the account is shown at the top left of every page, next to the logo. For this role it reads MODERATOR. In the sidebar, the menu is named Requirements Reviewer Submenu.

6.1 The Dashboard

The first page is the Moderators Dashboard, titled Building Permit. It has three cards. Each card has a switch, a search box and, for Permits For Printing, a number in a blue circle that counts the permits waiting. Click the switch of a card to show or hide its list. Click Scan QR at the top right to open a permit by scanning its QR code.

  • Permits For Printing: the building permits that the Building Official approved. See Section 6.3.
  • Declined Permits: the permits that were declined. Its columns are Date, Owner, Project Title, Location and Actions.
  • Permit For Occupancy: the occupancy permit requests. Its columns are Date, Application No., Project Title, Completion Date, Status, Attachment and Action.

A list that has nothing to show says No record found.

6.2 The sidebar

Under Requirements Reviewer Submenu, the sidebar has two pages:

  • Processed Permits: the page Processed Building Permit. It lists the building permits that you processed, with the Date, the Application No., the Application Type, the Owner and the Action. Type a number or a name in the search box to find one.
  • Processed Permits For Occupancy: the page Processed Permit For Occupancy. It lists the occupancy permits that you processed, with the Date, the Application No., the Permit Status, the Owner and the Action.

6.3 Permits for printing

Click the switch of Permits For Printing to show the approved permits. Type a number, a type or a name in the search box to find one. Click the arrows beside Date, Owner and Location to sort the list. The columns are Date, Owner, Project Title, TIS OR, TIS OR Date, Location and Actions. TIS OR and TIS OR Date show the number and the date of the official receipt of the payment. They stay empty until the payment is recorded in BPCOS.

Figure 6.5. The list Permits For Printing. The tooltip of an icon appears when you point to it.

6.3.1 The action icons

Each permit has six actions. They are labeled in the figure and explained below. An action that is not available is dimmed.

Figure 6.6. The actions of a permit, from left to right.
  1. Remarks (the arrow icon): opens the window Permit Remarks. See Section 6.3.2.
  2. Rollback to OBO (the circular arrows): sends the permit back to the Office of the Building Official. See Section 6.3.3.
  3. Attachment (the paperclip): opens the requirements that the applicant uploaded.
  4. FSEC (the button): records the Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance. See Section 6.3.4. It is enabled only when the Building Official approved the permit and no FSEC is recorded yet.
  5. Upload FSEC Certificate (the cloud icon): uploads the certificate of the FSEC. It is dimmed until an FSEC is added. Its tooltip then reads Add FSEC first. See Section 6.3.5.
  6. Print (the button): prints the building permit. It is dimmed until the certificate is uploaded. Its tooltip then reads Upload FSEC certificate to print this permit. See Section 6.3.6.

6.3.2 Remarks

Click the arrow icon to read the remarks of the permit. Each remark has its Date, the office in Process From, the Remarks and the Status. For an approved permit, the Building Official wrote Approved For Printing. Click the X to close the window.

Figure 6.7. The remarks of a permit for printing.

6.3.3 Rollback to OBO

Click the circular arrows to send the permit back to the Building Official, for example when it must be corrected. Pointing to the icon shows the tooltip Rollback to OBO.

Figure 6.8. The icon Rollback to OBO and its tooltip.

The window asks Are you sure to rollback this to OBO? Click Yes, Continue to send the permit back, or Cancel to keep it.

Figure 6.9. The confirmation window.

The green message Building permit successfully rollback to OBO. appears at the bottom left, and the permit leaves the list of Permits For Printing. The approval of the Building Official and the approval of the requirements are cleared, so the permit is no longer approved. It then appears again for the Building Official in the list of the Building Official (see Section 4.5, Approving or declining the permit), where it must be reviewed and approved again before it returns to the Moderator.

Figure 6.10. After the rollback. The message is boxed.

6.3.4 Adding the FSEC

Click FSEC. The window New FSEC opens with the project title and the selected occupancy of the permit, which come from the application. Fill in the other boxes. All of them are required:

  • FSEC No.: the number of the Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance.
  • OR Number: the number of the official receipt.
  • Date Issued: the date of issue, which starts with the date of today.

Click Save. The message FSEC successfully added. appears. Click No to leave without saving.

Figure 6.11. The window New FSEC. FSEC No., OR Number and Save are boxed.

After the save, the window closes and the green message FSEC successfully added. appears at the bottom left. In the row of that permit, the FSEC button is dimmed, because the FSEC is already recorded, and the cloud icon turns blue, so the certificate can now be uploaded.

Figure 6.12. After the FSEC is added. The message is boxed.

6.3.5 Uploading the FSEC certificate

The cloud icon is dimmed until an FSEC is added to the permit. Pointing to it then shows Add FSEC first. The system treats a permit as having an FSEC when its FSEC number is filled in. After you save the FSEC in Section 6.3.4, the cloud icon turns blue and its tooltip reads Upload FSEC CERTIFICATE.

Click the cloud icon. The window Upload FSEC Certificate opens with the note Upload the FSEC certificate file(s) for this permit. Drop the file into the dotted box, or click browse to choose it from your computer. The file can be an image, a PDF or a Word document, and you can add more than one. The chosen files are listed in the window. Click Remove beside a file to take it out before the upload. Click Upload to send the files, or Cancel to close the window without sending.

  • If you click Upload with no file, the message Please select at least one certificate file. appears and nothing is sent.
  • After a successful upload, a green message at the bottom left reads FSEC certificate uploaded successfully. The list refreshes.
  • The certificate is saved with the FSEC record of the permit. The permit counts as having a certificate once at least one file is saved.
Figure 6.13. The window Upload FSEC Certificate. browse and Upload are boxed.
Figure 6.14. After the upload. The message is boxed, and the Print button of that permit is now enabled.

6.3.6 Printing the permit

The Print button is dimmed until the FSEC certificate is uploaded. Pointing to it then shows Upload FSEC certificate to print this permit. This means the Moderator cannot print a permit before the FSEC is added and its certificate is uploaded. Once the certificate is uploaded, the tooltip reads Print Permit and the button turns blue.

  1. Click Print on the row of the permit. The building permit opens below that row.
  2. Check the details of the permit. See the list below.
  3. Click Print Permit at the bottom of the permit. Click Cancel instead to close the permit without printing.
  4. The print window of your browser opens. Choose your printer, or choose PDF at the bottom to save the permit as a file. Leave Print backgrounds ticked so the logos and the signature are printed. Click Print.

The preview may show two pages, where the second is almost empty. To print only the permit, choose Range from 1 to 1.

6.3.6.1 What the printed permit shows

The permit comes from the Office of the Building Official. The system fills it in from the application and the records of the permit:

  • Application Type and the ticked box above it, such as New.
  • Building Permit No. and Date Issued.
  • FSEC No. and its Date Issued, from the FSEC you added. They read None if no FSEC is recorded.
  • Official Receipt No. and Date Paid, from the payment record. They read N/A if no payment is recorded.
  • The owner, the project title, the location, the use and class of occupancy, the scope of work, the total project cost and the professional in charge of the construction.
  • A QR code at the top right, and the signature of the Building Official under Permit Issued By.
End of the workflow. After the Moderator prints the building permit, the building permit is completely processed. This ends the building permit workflow.
