Section 5

The Treasury

The Treasury receives the payment of the fees of the permit and issues the official receipt (OR). This is done in a separate system of the Municipality, OTIS, on the page Cashier. This section shows how the cashier issues the OR for a building permit.

Account. The page shows the label Cashier at the top left. The cashier signs in to OTIS with an account of the Treasury, not with an account of BPCOS. The cashier finds the assessment of the application from BPCOS. Saving the receipt in OTIS does not change the status of the application in BPCOS.

5.1 The Cashier page

The sidebar has three menus and Exit, which signs you out. Collection opens a list of the receipts that the cashier issues: Issuance of Official Receipt(OR), Business Permit(OR), Building Permit, Community Tax Certificate(CTC) - Ind, Community Tax Certificate(CTC) - Corp, Burial Permit and Fee Receipt and Online Request. The other menus are Process and Reports. For a building permit, click Collection, then Building Permit.

The page Building Permit has these parts:

  • OR No. and the button Check, at the top left.
  • Select OR To Print, at the top right.
  • Template List: the fees that are paid. Its columns are #, Acct Code, Particulars and Amount, with the Total under it.
  • Check List: for a payment by check. Its columns are Check No., Check Date, Bank Code, Check Amount and Action. The button Add Check adds a check.
Figure 5.1. The page Building Permit of the cashier. The label Cashier is boxed.

5.2 Issuing an official receipt

The applicant pays the fees of the assessment at the Treasury. The cashier then issues the OR in these steps.

5.2.1 Step 1. Type the OR number

Type the number of the next OR in OR No. and click Check. The system checks the number.

Figure 5.2. The OR number is typed. Check is boxed.

When the number is accepted, the page shows the details of the receipt:

  • Payor: empty until you retrieve the application.
  • Document Date and OR Date: the date, and the date and time, of the receipt.
  • Add Details: finds the application that is paid. See the next step.
  • The box at the right: the Liq Officer, the Collector, the Form Type, which is the accountable form, and the RCD No. and RCLO No.

Save is dimmed until an application is retrieved.

Figure 5.3. The details of the receipt. Add Details is boxed.

5.2.2 Step 2. Find the application

Click Add Details. The window Filter To Display opens. Choose how to search by checking one option, type the value, then click Filter:

  • Search for Application No.: type the Application No. of the permit.
  • Search for Owner: type the name of the owner in Owner. This box is required.

Filter is dimmed until you check an option and type the value.

Figure 5.4. The window Filter To Display. The two ways to search are boxed.
Figure 5.5. Search for Owner. The name is typed and Filter is boxed.

5.2.3 Step 3. Retrieve the record

The window lists the applications that match, each with its Application No. and the name of the owner. Check that it is the right application, then click Retrieve to confirm it.

Figure 5.6. The matching application. Retrieve is boxed.

The page fills in. The Payor is the owner. The Template List shows the fees of the assessment, with the account code of each and the Total to be paid. At this stage the particulars read FOR BUILDING / STRUCTURE (OBO). If the owner pays by check, click Add Check and fill in the check, see Section 5.3. Save is now enabled.

Figure 5.7. The retrieved record. Save is boxed.

5.2.4 Step 4. Save the receipt

Click Save. The message Building permit created successfully appears and the window Next OR? asks what you want to do. Click Yes to start the next OR. Click No to stay on this receipt, for example to print it.

Figure 5.8. The window Next OR? appears after the receipt is saved. No and Yes are boxed.

When you stay on the receipt, the button Print OR appears beside Select OR To Print. The particulars of the fees now show their names, for example Accessory Structure Fees and Electrical Fees.

Figure 5.9. The saved receipt. Print OR is boxed.

5.2.5 Step 5. Print the receipt

Click Print OR. The print window of the browser opens with the receipt. Choose a printer, or save it as a PDF, then click Print. The receipt has the OR number and date, the office of the Treasury, the name of the payor, each fee with its account code and amount, the total in figures and in words, and the name of the treasurer.

Figure 5.10. The print window of the browser.
Figure 5.11. The printed official receipt.

5.3 Paying by check

When the owner pays by check, click Add Check in the Check List, before you save the receipt. The window that opens has these fields: Check No., Check Date, which starts with the date of today, Bank Code and Check Amount. Fill them in and click Create to add the check to the list. Click Close to leave without adding it.

Figure 5.12. The window to add a check. Create and Add Check are boxed.

5.4 Printing a saved receipt

To print a receipt that was issued before, click Select OR To Print at the top right. The window Select OR to print lists the saved receipts, newest first, with the OR Date, the OR No., the Payor and the Business, which is empty for a building permit. Type an OR number, a payor or a business in the search box to find one. Click Select beside the receipt, then click Print OR, as in Step 5.

Figure 5.13. The window Select OR to print. The search box and Select are boxed.
Status in BPCOS. Saving the receipt does not update the status of the application in BPCOS. The application keeps the status it had before payment.

5.5 Messages you may see

While you issue a receipt, the page may show one of the messages below. They do not harm the record. Read the message, then follow the steps.

5.5.1 No record found

After you click Filter in the window Filter To Display, a red message, No record found, appears at the bottom left. It means that no application matches what you typed, so the Template List stays empty and the Check List says No record found. The most common causes are:

  • The name or the application number is typed wrongly. Check the spelling and try again.
  • The owner has not been assessed or approved yet, so there is nothing to pay. The application must first be assessed and approved by the Building Official.
  • The wrong way to search is checked. Check Search for Application No. to type a number, or Search for Owner to type a name, not both.

Click Add Details again and search with a shorter name, such as the last name, or with the application number of the applicant.

Figure 5.14. The message No record found. It is boxed.

5.5.2 O.R # already used

When you type an OR number and click Check, the system looks it up. If a receipt with that number was already issued, the window O.R # ALREADY USED! appears. Click OK to close it. The page then shows the receipt that already uses the number: its payor, the fees in the Template List and the date. The buttons Select OR To Print, Mark As Cancelled and Print OR appear at the top right, and Add Details is dimmed, so you cannot save another receipt with the same number.

Do not issue the receipt with this number. Do one of these:

  • Type the next unused OR number in OR No. and click Check again.
  • Click Print OR if you only need another copy of the receipt that was already issued, see Section 5.4.
Figure 5.15. The window O.R # already used. The message and OK are boxed.

5.6 Reports: Abstract of Collection

Click Reports in the sidebar to open its menu. It has Accountable Forms and Collection. Click Collection to show three reports: Abstract of Collection, Report of Collection and Deposit(RCD) and Report of Collection by Liquidating Officer(RCLO). Click Abstract of Collection.

The Abstract of Collection lists the official receipts that were issued in a period. The page is empty, with a Total of 0.00, until you choose what to display. At the top right are three buttons:

  • Filter: chooses what to display.
  • Print: prints the report.
  • Export To Excel: saves the report as a spreadsheet.
Figure 5.16. The Abstract of Collection before a filter. Filter is boxed.

5.6.1 Choosing what to display

Click Filter. In the window Filter To Display, check the filters that you need and fill in their boxes:

  • Filter By Date: it is checked at the start. Set Date & Time From and Date & Time To. Click the calendar icon of a box to open a calendar, click the day, then click OK. Click Cancel to close the calendar.
  • Filter By Collector: choose a Collector from the list.
  • Filter By Fund: choose a Fund from the list.
  • Filter By OR Template: choose an OR Template from the list.

Click Filter to show the report.

Figure 5.17. The window Filter To Display. Filter is boxed.
Figure 5.18. The calendar of a date box.

5.6.2 Reading the report

The report has the heading Republic of the Philippines, Municipal Government of Oas, the title Abstract of Collection and the period, written For the period of the first date to the last date. The table has the columns OR Date, OR No., Gender, Payee, Account Code, Particulars and Amount. Each receipt has one row for each fee that it collected, with the account code of the fee.

The rows are grouped by the accountable form of the receipts, written FORM, for example AF#51. After each group, the report shows the Sub Total for the month and the Sub Total for the form, and at the end of the report, the Total. The building permit receipts of Section 5.2 are listed here under AF#51, with their fees: Electrical Fees and Accessory Structure Fees.

Figure 5.19. The Abstract of Collection for a period. The payees are covered.
