Introduction

This manual explains how to use the Building Permit and Certificate of Occupancy System (BPCOS) of the Municipality of Oas, Albay.

In this section

  • 1.2 Opening BPCOS
  • 1.3 The sign in page
  • 1.4 How this manual is organized

1.1 About BPCOS

BPCOS is one of the online applications of the OAS Unified Online Services. It is for builders and developers. The system covers permit applications, compliance verification, inspections and occupancy certification, in line with the National Building Code.

What the system is used for, step by step, will be added when the process and the roles are documented.

1.2 Opening BPCOS

  1. Open a web browser (for example Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Safari).
  2. Go to https://bpcos.oas.lguapp.com. You can also start from the OAS home page (https://oas.lguapp.com): scroll to LGU Online Applications and click the card Building Permit & Certificate of Occupancy.
  3. The sign in page of BPCOS opens.
Screenshot of the OAS home page card and of the sign in page.

1.3 The sign in page

The sign in page has the title Sign in and the line Sign in on the internal platform. It has these parts:

  • Email Address and Password: the details of your account. The eye icon in the password box shows or hides the password.
  • Log In: signs you in.
  • Create new account: opens the form to create an account.
  • Forget password?: starts the recovery of a forgotten password.
  • How to's (Guide): a link to the guides of the system.

The first time you open the page, a notice about cookies appears at the bottom. Click I Agree to close it. A small message, Settings Updated, may also appear at the top right. Click Close to close it.

Screenshot of the sign in page, with the cookie notice and the Settings Updated message.
