2.1 The Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page after you sign in. In the middle, it shows the logo of the Municipality, the Contact Details of the office (Email and Contact Number) and a How to line with the link to the FAQs.

Below is the card Building Permit. Click it to open the list of your building permit requests.

Figure 2.1. The applicant Dashboard.

2.2 The sidebar

The sidebar is on the left of every page. Click Dashboard to go back to the Dashboard. Under Sub Menu, click Building Permit to start a new request, Permit List to open List (your building permit requests) or Draft (requests you saved but did not send), and Permit For Payment to see the permits that are ready for payment. On a small screen, click the menu icon at the top left to show or hide the sidebar.

Figure 2.2. The sidebar with Permit List open.

2.3 The top bar

The top bar is at the top of every page. At the left are the menu icon, the logo of the Municipality and the label APPLICANT, which tells you the role of your account. At the right are the notification bell, the gear icon for Settings (layout, text size and theme) and your name. Click your name to open Account, where you change your profile and your password, or Logout to sign out.

Figure 2.3. The left side of the top bar.

2.4 Account settings

The page Account Settings has two tabs. In General, you edit your profile and click Save Changes. In Security, you type a new password twice and click Change Password.

2.5 New building permit application

A building permit application goes through these parts: Permit Request, Your building permit requests, Requirements, Application Review, Sent to Building Official, Approved, Application Paid and Building Permit Released.

2.5.1 Permit Request

You can start a building permit request in two ways.

From the sidebar. Click Building Permit in the sidebar. The page Building Permit Request opens.

From the Dashboard.

  1. On the Dashboard, click the Building Permit card. The page List of Building Permits opens. A new account has no request yet, so the table says No record found.
  2. Click New Application at the top right. The page Building Permit Request opens.
Figure 2.4. Click the Building Permit card on the Dashboard.
Figure 2.5. Click New Application on the List of Building Permits.

The request is a form with four steps. The step bar at the top of the form names them, and the step you are on is shown in bold blue:

  1. Owner/Applicant Details
  2. Structure Profile
  3. Full Time Inspectors
  4. Summary

2.5.1.1 Moving between steps

The buttons at the bottom of every step are the navigation controls:

  • Next: goes to the next step. The system first checks the required fields of the step. If one is empty, the form stays on the step and shows a red message.
  • Previous: goes back to the step before, so you can check or change what you typed. It is dimmed on step 1.
  • The four dots between the buttons show which step you are on. The filled dot is the current step.

On the last step, Next is replaced by Save As Draft. See Step 4.

Figure 2.6. Previous and Next (boxed) at the bottom of a step. The dots show the current step.

2.5.1.2 Required fields

Important. A field with an asterisk (*) in its name is required. The page reminds you: MUST READ! All fields with (*) is required. Click Next only after you fill in all of them.

When a required field is empty, a red message appears under it and the field is outlined in red. Fill it in, then click Next again. Messages you may see:

  • Select at least one scope of work. (step 2)
  • Use or Character of occupancy is required. (step 2)
  • No. of units is a required field. (step 2, and the same wording for the other required fields)
Figure 2.7. Step 1. The required fields are boxed in red.
Figure 2.8. Step 2. The required fields and messages are boxed in red.

2.5.1.3 Step 1. Owner/Applicant Details

This step is about the applicant and the project. Fill in these fields. The first five are required.

  • Application Type *: click the box and choose one of the types below.
  • Lastname *, Firstname *, Middle Name and TIN No.
  • For construction owned by an enterprise *: choose Yes or No.
  • Form of ownership *: for example Private.
  • Address No., Street, Barangay, City/Municipality and Zip Code of the owner.
  • Contact No.
  • Project Title: the name of the project, for example a two-storey residential home.

The types of Application Type are: Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance, Amendatory, Complex, Renewal, Locational Clearance, New and Simple.

Figure 2.9. The choices of Application Type.
Figure 2.10. Step 1 filled in. Click Next to go to step 2.

2.5.1.4 Step 2. Structure Profile

This step describes the building and where it will be built. It has four parts.

Location of Construction

  • Lot No., Block No., TCT No. and Current Tax Dec. No.
  • Street
  • Barangay * and City/Municipality Of *: click each box and choose from the list.

Scope of Work

Check at least one of: New Construction, Erection, Addition, Alteration, Renovation, Conversion, Repair, Moving, Raising, Accessory Building/Structure, Legalization of Existing Building, Others. If you check Others, type the work in the Specify box.

Figure 2.11. Location of Construction and Scope of Work filled in.

Use or Character of Occupancy *

Check the box that matches how the building will be used. This part is required. The choices are grouped:

  • Group A, Residential (Dwellings): Single, Duplex, Residential R-1, R-2.
  • Group B, Residential: Hotel, Motel, Townhouse, Dormitory, Residential R-3, R-4, R-5, Boarding House or Lodging House.
  • Group C, Educational and Recreational: School Building, School Auditorium or Gymnasium, Civic Center, Church, Mosque, Temple or Chapel, Club House.
  • Group D, Institutional: Hospital or Similar Structure, Home for the Aged, Government Office.
  • Group E, Commercial: Bank, Store, Shopping Center or Mall Establishment, Drinking or Dining, Shop.
  • Group F, Light Industrial: Factory or Plant using incombustible, non-explosive materials.
  • Group G, Medium Industrial: Storage or Warehouse, and Factory, for hazardous or highly flammable materials.
  • Group H, Assembly (occupant load less than 1,000): Theater, Auditorium, Conventional Hall, Grandstand or Bleacher.
  • Group I, Assembly (occupant load 1,000 or more): Coliseum, Sports Complex, Convention Center and similar structure.
  • Group J-1, Agricultural: Barn, Granary, Poultry House, Piggery, Grain Meal, Grain Silo.
  • Group J-2, Accessories: Private Carport, Garage, Tower, Swimming Pool, Fence over 1.8 m, Steel, Concrete or Tank.

Each group also has an Others box with a Specify line for a use that is not listed.

Figure 2.12. The groups of Use or Character of Occupancy.

Size, dates and costs

  • Occupancy Classified
  • No. of units * and No. of Storey *
  • Total Floor Area * and Lot Area *
  • Proposed date of construction
  • Total Estimated Cost *
  • The costs of the parts: Building Cost, Electrical Cost, Mechanical Cost, Electronics Cost and Plumbing Cost.
  • The costs of the equipment installed: Electrical Equipment Cost, Mechanical Equipment Cost, Electronics Equipment Cost and Plumbing Equipment Cost.
  • Expected Date of completion

If a required field here is empty, for example No. of units, it turns red and shows a message.

Figure 2.13. A required field left empty is outlined in red with a message.

2.5.1.5 Step 3. Full Time Inspectors

This step names the full time inspector and supervisor of the construction works, who represents the owner, and signs for the applicant.

  • Architect or Civil Engineer *: the name of the architect or civil engineer. This is required. The note under the box says the engineer signs and seals over the printed name.
  • Address, PRC No., Validity, PTR No., Date Issued, Issued At and TIN of the engineer.
  • Applicant: the name of the applicant is filled in from step 1 and cannot be changed here. Set the Date.
  • With my consent: lot owner or authorized representative: type the name in Lot Owner Or Representative and set the Date.
  • For the applicant and for the lot owner or representative: Address, Gov't Issued ID No., Date Issued and Place Issued.
Figure 2.14. Step 3. The Architect or Civil Engineer field is required.
Figure 2.15. Step 3 filled in.

2.5.1.6 Step 4. Summary

The Summary shows everything you typed on the Unified Application Form for Building Permit, as it will be printed. Read it carefully. To change something, click Previous until you reach the step, change it, then click Next again. The form is made of boxes:

  • Box 1: the owner or applicant, the location of construction, the scope of work and the use or character of occupancy, with the size and the costs.
  • Box 2: the full time inspector and supervisor of construction works.
  • Box 3: the applicant. Box 4: the lot owner or authorized representative.
  • Box 5: the notary section. The line This portion is need to be notarize tells you that this part is signed before a notary public.
Figure 2.16. The top of the Summary, Box 1.
Figure 2.17. Boxes 2 to 5 of the Summary.

At the bottom of the Summary:

  • Print: prints the form, so that you can sign it and have Box 5 notarized.
  • Previous: goes back to step 3.
  • Save As Draft: saves the application. The message Request successfully sent. appears and the Requirements List opens.
Figure 2.18. Print, Previous and Save As Draft (boxed) at the bottom of the Summary.

2.5.2 Your building permit requests

Click Permit List in the sidebar, then List, to open the page List of Building Permits. It shows every building permit application you have started. A new application appears here right after you click Save As Draft on the Summary.

Figure 2.19. The List of Building Permits page.

2.5.2.1 The list

Click New Application at the top right to start another request. See 3.5 New building permit application. The table shows the Reference Number, the Building Permit No (a dash until the permit is issued), the Applicant/Owner, the Project Title, the Date Created, the Status Of Application (a new application is DRAFT) and the Date Approved. At the right of each row are two icons: the arrow and the three dots. The bottom of the table has Rows per page and the page count.

Figure 2.20. The arrow (top) and the three dots (bottom) at the right of a row.

2.5.2.2 Application remarks

Click the arrow to open Building Permit Remarks. The offices that process your application write their remarks here. The table has the columns Date, Process From, Remarks and Status. A new application has no remarks yet, so the table says No remarks found. Click the X to close the window.

Figure 2.21. The Building Permit Remarks window.

2.5.2.3 More options

Click the three dots to open a menu of what you can do with the application. An option that is not available for the current status is dimmed.

Figure 2.22. The menu of the three dots.

2.5.2.4 Details

Click Details to read the application as the Unified Application Form for Building Permit. Besides Boxes 1 to 5, it has Box 6, to be accomplished by the Processing and Evaluation Division, with the table Assessed Fees, Account Code, Amount Due and Assessed By. Until the fees are assessed, it says No assessment found. The buttons are:

  • Close: goes back to the list.
  • Update Details: dimmed in this view. To change the application, use Edit.
  • Print: prints the form.
Figure 2.23. The Details of an application, with Close and Print boxed.

2.5.2.5 Edit

Click Edit to change an application. It is available only while the application is a DRAFT. The page Update Building Permit Request shows the whole form with your answers, so you can change them. Fields with an asterisk (*) are required.

Figure 2.24. The top of the Update Building Permit Request page.

At the bottom of the page, click Update Request to save your changes, or Cancel to leave without saving.

Figure 2.25. Update Request and Cancel at the bottom of the page.

2.5.3 Requirements

The page Requirements List opens after you click Save As Draft on the Summary. It is where you attach the documents that support your application. You can also open it later: in the List of Building Permits, click the three dots of the application, then Requirements.

Figure 2.26. Opening the Requirements from the list of building permits.

The box Attach Documents lists six groups of documents. Click a group to show its requirements at the right. Three buttons are at the top of the page:

  • Update Details: goes back to the application form, so you can change what you typed.
  • Save As Draft: keeps the application as a draft and opens the Permit List. You can finish it later.
  • Proceed to Permitting: sends the application for review. It can be clicked only after all the required files are attached.
Figure 2.27. The Requirements List. The three buttons are boxed.

Each requirement shows two tags. NO FILES means that you have not attached a file yet. OPTIONAL means that the requirement may be skipped. A requirement that is not optional must have a file before you can click Proceed to Permitting. Click More beside a requirement to attach its file.

The box Attach Documents has six groups of documents to upload: Forms, Plans, Technical Documents, PRC IDs & PTRs, Personal Documents and Certifications. Fill in and sign the forms, scan them and upload the scans. The Unified Application Form is the Summary of step 4: print it, sign it and have Box 5 notarized.

2.5.3.1 Uploading a requirement

  1. Click More beside the requirement, then click Upload.
  2. In the window Upload Requirement, drop the file into the box, or click browse to choose it. For a plan of several pages, upload a PDF.
  3. Type your note in Remarks. This box is required.
  4. Click Save. After the upload, the tag of the requirement changes from NO FILES to 1 FILES UPLOADED. Click More and View Files to see the files again.
Figure 2.28. The menu of More. Upload is boxed.
Figure 2.29. The Upload Requirement window. Remarks and Save are boxed.

2.5.3.2 Sending the requirements

When every requirement that is not optional has a file, click Proceed to Permitting. The application is sent for review, and its status in the List of Building Permits changes to SENT TO OBO. The next two parts explain who reviews it: first the Reviewer, then the Building Official.

Figure 2.30. The status after the requirements are sent.

2.5.4 Application Review

After you click Proceed to Permitting, the application is sent to the Reviewer. The Reviewer is an office of the Municipality that reviews the requirements you submitted. The Reviewer opens the files of each requirement, checks them, and then sets the application as review started. The pages of the Reviewer are in Section 3.

You do not need to do anything while the application is under review. The only change that you see is the status of the application in the List of Building Permits: it changes to SENT TO OBO.

Figure 2.31. The status of the application is SENT TO OBO.

2.5.5 Sent to Building Official

The status SENT TO OBO means that the application is with the Office of the Building Official (OBO), the engineering office of the Municipality. This status is the only change that you see on your side while the application is with the OBO. The OBO decides on the application. It does these things:

  • Receives the application. The application appears in the Building Permit list of the Building Official, with its remarks and its attachments, so the Building Official can read what you submitted and what the Reviewer wrote.
  • Assesses the fees. The Building Official prepares the assessment of the fees that you must pay for the permit.
  • Approves or declines the application. The Building Official gives the decision and writes the reason in the remarks.
  • Prints the permit. When the fees are paid, the Building Official prints the building permit.

The pages of the Building Official are in Section 4.

2.5.6 Approved

When the Building Official approves the permit, the status of your application in the List of Building Permits changes from SENT TO OBO to APPROVED, and the Building Permit No that the Building Official typed is shown in its column. The bell at the top of the page also shows a notification. The pages of the Building Official are in Section 4.5.

Figure 2.32. The status of the application is APPROVED, with its Building Permit No.

2.5.6.1 Reading the remarks

Click the arrow icon of the application to read the remarks of the Building Official. Each remark has its date, the office in Process From, which is Approver, and the Remarks. After approval, you can see the note of the approval and a second note, Approved For Printing, which tells you that the permit was sent for printing.

Figure 2.33. The remarks of the approved application.

2.5.6.2 Viewing the assessment and paying

In the menu of the three dots, click Assessments to open the page Building Permit Assessment. It lists the assessed fees with their Account Code, the Amount Due of each, who assessed them, and the TOTAL that you must pay. Click Dashboard to go back to the Dashboard.

Pay at the Treasury. Take note of the total and pay the fees at the Treasury of the Municipality, the cashier. The cashier issues your official receipt, see Section 5. The button Online Payment is not available at this time.
Figure 2.34. The Building Permit Assessment page.

2.5.7 Application Paid

After your fees are paid at the Treasury, the status of your application in the List of Building Permits changes from APPROVED to APPLICATION PAID, shown in green. The row keeps the Reference Number and the Building Permit No. The permit then goes to the Moderator, who adds the FSEC, uploads its certificate and prints the permit, see Section 6. You can then view the permit, see below.

Figure 2.35. The status of the application is APPLICATION PAID.

2.5.7.1 Viewing the permit

Once your fees are paid, the permit can be viewed. In the menu of the three dots, click View Permit. Before payment, View Permit cannot be opened. Edit stays dimmed.

Figure 2.36. The menu of an approved application. View Permit is boxed.

The page shows the Building Permit of the Office of the Building Official. It has the type of application, the Building Permit No., the date issued and the FSEC number, then the owner, the project title, the location, the use of the building, the scope of work, the total project cost and the professional in charge of the construction. At the bottom is the name and the signature of the Building Official who issued it. Official Receipt No. and Date Paid show the receipt of your payment. Print Permit can be used only after the Moderator has uploaded the FSEC certificate and printed the permit, see Section 6. Until then, you can view the permit but not print it. Click Print Permit to print the permit. Click Cancel to go back.

Figure 2.37. The Building Permit. Print Permit is boxed.

2.5.8 Building Permit Released

Important. The building permit is released only when the BPCOS Moderator has uploaded the FSEC certificate and printed the Building Permit. Until then, you can view the permit but you cannot print it.

Once the Moderator has done both, the building permit is complete and your application ends. You can open View Permit again and click Print Permit to print your copy. The steps of the Moderator are in Section 6.
