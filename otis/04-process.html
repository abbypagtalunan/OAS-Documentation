Section 4

Process

The Process menu closes the collections of a period. RCD Processing gathers the receipts of one collector into a Report of Collection and Deposits (RCD). RCLO Processing then gathers the RCDs of a fund into a Report of Collection by Liquidating Officer (RCLO). Each step gives the report a number.

Reading the colored boxes. Messages of TIS are shown in boxes: gray for a question or notice, green for success, red for an error and yellow for a warning.

4.1 The Process menu and the order of work

Open the sidebar and click Process. It has two pages: Report of Collection and Deposits(RCD) Processing and Report of Collection by Liquidating Officer(RCLO) Processing.

Figure 4.1. The sidebar with Process open. 1 The two Process pages. The name and email are blurred.

The two steps follow each other:

  1. Receipts are issued on the Transaction pages (Section 3.2). Each receipt comes from a booklet of 50 (Section 2.6.1).
  2. RCD Processing (Section 4.2) takes the receipts of one collector, one fund and one kind of form in a range of dates, adds up the money and gives them an RCD No.
  3. RCLO Processing (Section 4.3) takes the RCDs of one fund in a range of dates and gives them an RCLO No.

Both pages also feed the reports of the Reports menu.

4.2 RCD Processing

This page processes the receipts of one collector into an RCD. When it opens, it shows a table and two icons at the top right: the folder icon with a plus sign opens the window PROCESS RCD DIALOG, and the magnifying glass opens the list of RCDs that were already made. The table has no row until you retrieve one, and says No record found.

Figure 4.2. The RCD Processing page when it opens. 1 The folder icon: process an RCD · 2 The magnifying glass: the list of RCDs · 3 The table.

4.2.1 The Process RCD window

Click the folder icon. Fill in the boxes of PROCESS RCD DIALOG from top to bottom:

CollectorRequired. A list of the collectors, each shown as the name and the code. The list follows your assigned personnel (Section 1.2.6): only the collectors of type Collector that are assigned to you appear.

The two buttons at the bottom are gray until the required boxes are filled in. Retrieve shows the receipts. Process makes the RCD.

4.2.2 Retrieve: read before you process

Click Retrieve. TIS fills the table of the page behind the window. It only reads, it does not make the RCD. Check the table first. The columns are:

  • Collector and, under Issued, the booklet that was given to the collector: Qty, From and To. Every booklet has 50 receipts.
  • Under Sold: how many receipts were used (Qty) and the first and last number that were used (From and To).
  • Collected Amount: the money collected with those receipts.
  • Suffix, Fromno and Tono: the receipt suffix and the first and last number of the booklet.
  • Balance: the receipts that were not used yet (50 minus the Qty sold).

The total of the Collected Amount is shown below the table. For example, one booklet of 50 receipts, number 4099451 to 4099500, shows 49 sold from 4099452 to 4099500, an amount of 5,310.00 and a balance of 1.

Figure 4.6. The table after Retrieve. 1 Issued: the booklet · 2 Sold: the receipts that were used · 3 Collected Amount · 4 Balance. The collector names are blurred.

4.2.3 Process

When the table is right, click Process in the window. TIS gives the RCD a number and fills the RCD No. box. The number is made of the fund code, the collector code, the RCD date and a running number, for example 100(01)JRR10212026029: fund 100(01), collector JRR, 10/21/2026, number 029. RCD No. updated successfully appears.

Process then marks every receipt of the range (and its checks) with the RCD No. and the RCD date, so that the same receipt cannot go into another RCD. Receipts of other collectors, other funds or other forms are not touched.

Caution. Process changes many receipts at once. Retrieve first and check the dates, the fund and the collector.

4.2.4 The list of RCDs and Remove

Click the magnifying glass to open the RCD List. It shows the RCD Date, the RCD No. and the RCLO No. of each RCD, with a Remove button. Type in Search RCD No. to find one. When there is none, No RCD record appears.

  • An RCD that is already in an RCLO shows its RCLO No. and its Remove button is gray, so it cannot be removed.
  • An RCD without an RCLO No. can be removed. TIS asks Are you sure to delete this RCD? Yes removes the RCD, and the receipts of that RCD become free again for a new RCD. RCD deleted successfully follows.
Figure 4.7. The RCD List. 1 Search RCD No. · 2 The RCD No. of each RCD · 3 The RCLO No. of each RCD (empty when the RCD is not in an RCLO yet).

4.3 RCLO Processing

This page gathers RCDs into an RCLO. It looks like RCD Processing: a table, the folder icon (PROCESS RCLO DIALOG) and the magnifying glass (the list of RCLOs). Its columns are RCD No., Liquidating Officer, Collector, Money Value and RCD Date.

Figure 4.8. The RCLO Processing page when it opens. 1 The folder icon: process an RCLO · 2 The magnifying glass: the list of RCLOs · 3 The table.

4.3.1 The Process RCLO window

Click the folder icon. The window PROCESS RCLO DIALOG has fewer boxes than the RCD window:

  • Fund Code (required): the fund of the RCDs.
  • From RCD Date and To RCD Date: the first and the last date of the RCDs. Both start as today.
  • RCLO Date: the date of the RCLO. It starts as today.
  • RCLO No.: empty until you click Process.
  • Retrieve and Process, gray until a fund is chosen.
Figure 4.9. The Process RCLO window. 1 Fund Code · 2 From RCD Date, To RCD Date and RCLO Date · 3 RCLO No. · 4 Retrieve · 5 Process.

4.3.2 Retrieve and Process

RCD and RCLO are not the same report. RCD means Report of Collection and Deposit. RCLO means Report of Collection by Liquidating Officer. The RCD is the smaller report and the RCLO is built from RCDs.
RCD (Section 4.2)RCLO (Section 4.3)
Full nameReport of Collection and DepositReport of Collection by Liquidating Officer
What it processesThe official receipts of one collector, one fund and one kind of form, in a range of receipt dates (From OR Date and To OR Date).The RCDs of one fund, in a range of RCD dates (From RCD Date and To RCD Date). It does not look at single receipts.
What you chooseLiq Officer, Collector, Fund Code and Form Code.Fund Code only.
What it makesAn RCD No. (fund, collector code, date and a running number), for example 100(01)JRR09102026022.An RCLO No. (fund name, date and a running number), for example 100(01) GENERAL FUND09102026000010.
What is markedEvery receipt (and check) of the range receives the RCD No. and the RCD date.Every RCD of the range receives the RCLO No. and the RCLO date.
The result on the pageOne row for each booklet of the collector: issued, sold, collected amount and balance.One row for each RCD: its number, the officer, the collector, the money value and the date, with the total.
Who it coversOne collector.All the collectors of the liquidating officer under the fund.
Printed reportReport of Collection and Deposit (Section 5.4).Report of Collection by Liquidating Officer, with the deposits (Section 5.5).
OrderFirst.Second, after the RCDs exist. An RCD in an RCLO can no longer be removed.

Choose the fund and the first date, then click Retrieve. The page behind the window lists the RCDs that are not yet in an RCLO: the RCD No., the liquidating officer, the collector, the money value of each RCD and the RCD date, with the total below. In the example, one RCD, 100(01)JRR09102026022 of 09/10/2026, has a money value of 14,970.00.

Figure 4.10. The table after Retrieve. 1 The RCDs that are not in an RCLO yet, and their total. The officer and collector names are blurred.

When the table is right, click Process. TIS gives the RCLO a number (the fund name, the RCLO date and a running number, for example 100(01) GENERAL FUND10212026000014) and fills the RCLO No. box. RCLO no. updated successfully appears. Every RCD of the table receives the RCLO No. and the RCLO date.

Caution. Process makes the RCDs part of an RCLO and locks them: an RCD with an RCLO No. can no longer be removed from RCD Processing.

4.3.3 The list of RCLOs and Remove

Click the magnifying glass to open the RCLO List. It shows the RCLO Date and the RCLO No. of each RCLO, with a Remove button. Type in Search RCLO No. to find one. When there is none, No RCLO record appears. Remove asks Are you sure to delete this RCLO? Yes removes the RCLO and frees its RCDs, which can then go into another RCLO. RCLO deleted successfully follows.

Figure 4.11. The RCLO List. 1 Search RCLO No. · 2 The RCLO No. of each RCLO · 3 The Remove buttons.
The two reports. The printed reports of these numbers are in the Reports menu: Report of Collection and Deposit(RCD) and Report of Collection by Liquidating Officer(RCLO).
