Section 6

The Cashier

The Cashier is the person who receives payments at the treasury. A user with the role Cashier signs in and goes to the same workspace as the Admin, but the sidebar shows only the work of the cashier: Collection, Process and Reports. The Transaction group has no Disbursement page, and the Referential and Admin menus do not appear.

6.2 How the day of a cashier goes

  1. Receive the payment and make the receipt. Open the page of the payment (Collection). Type the receipt number from your booklet, click Check, fill in the payor and what is paid, and save (Section 3.2). Print the receipt.
  2. Online payments. Open Online Request, find the request and set its payment with the receipt number (Section 3.9).
  3. Close the collection. At the end of a period, process the RCD of your collector (Section 4.2). The RCLO is processed after the RCDs (Section 4.3).
  4. Print the reports of the Reports menu (Section 5) and deposit the money. The deposit is recorded on the RCLO report.

6.3 What limits a cashier

  • Assigned personnel. A receipt can be processed only when its collector is one of the officers assigned to the cashier's user. Otherwise TIS shows You are not allowed to proccess this personnel. The Collector list of RCD Processing offers only the assigned collectors (Section 1.2.6). Ask the Admin to assign the officers.
  • Assigned RPT users. The Import of RPT receipts in the RCD report offers only the RPT users assigned to the cashier (Section 1.2.5).
  • Erase. On the Business Permit page, the Erase button (and the Del key) is shown only to the Admin. A cashier can look at a business receipt but cannot erase it.
  • No set-up pages. The lists of the Referential menu (funds, accounts, collection types, officers, booklets and the OR templates) are kept by the Admin. If a collection type or an account is missing, ask the Admin.
How this section was made. The Cashier menu is read from the system code. The pages were viewed with an Admin account, because a Cashier sign-in was not available. A page may show a different list of collectors, because the lists follow the assigned personnel of the user who signs in.
