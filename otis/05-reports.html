Section 5

Reports

The Reports menu shows and prints the official reports of the treasury. Most of them are made from the receipts and numbers of the earlier sections: the forms report on the booklets, the Abstract of Collection reports on the receipts, and the RCD and RCLO reports show the numbers made in Process.

Reading the colored boxes. Messages of TIS are shown in boxes: gray for a question or notice, green for success, red for an error and yellow for a warning.

5.1 The Reports menu

Open the sidebar and click Reports. It has five reports in three groups: Accountable Forms (two reports), Collection (Abstract of Collection), and then Report of Collection and Deposit(RCD) and Report of Collection by Liquidating Officer(RCLO).

Figure 5.1. The sidebar with Reports open. 1 The Reports group. The name and email are blurred.

The pages use the same few buttons: Filter or Search opens a window to choose what to show, Print prints the report as it is on the page, and in the Abstract of Collection Export To Excel saves it as a spreadsheet. Choosing the filter only reads data. It does not change any receipt.

5.2 Accountable Forms

5.2.1 Report of Accountability for Accountable Forms

This report shows, for each booklet of accountable forms, what happened to it during the year. When it opens it shows the empty form: the title, Accountable Officer, Designation and Cost Center, and a table with the columns Form Code, Trans Date, Beginning Balance, Receipt, Issued, Returned or Transfer and Ending Balance. Each of the five groups has Qty., From and To.

Figure 5.2. Step 1. Click Filter (1). 1 Filter · 2 Print · 3 Accountable Officer, Designation and Cost Center · 4 The columns of the report.

Step 2. Choose what to show. The window Filter To Display has the Year (it starts as the present year) and the check box Filter By Collector. Tick the box to choose one collector from the list; leave it empty to show all collectors. Then click Filter in the window.

Figure 5.3. Step 2. The filter window. 1 Year · 2 Filter By Collector (ticked) · 3 The Collector list · 4 Filter. The collector name is blurred.

Step 3. Read the report. Each row is one booklet. In the example the first row is a booklet of 50 receipts of BIR#0016: how many receipts were issued and how many are left (the ending balance). The heading shows the accountable officer, the designation and the cost center. The data of the officer is blurred.

Figure 5.4. Step 3. The report after Filter. 1 Filter · 2 Print · 3 Accountable Officer, Designation and Cost Center · 4 One row for each booklet.

Below the table is the certification of the accountable officer, with the name and signature and the date.

Figure 5.5. The end of the report. 1 The certification, the name of the accountable officer and the date. The name is blurred.

Print. Click Print. TIS opens the print window of the browser with the report as it is on the page. Choose the printer, or Save as PDF, and print.

5.2.2 Consolidated Report of Accountability for Accountable Forms

This is the same report in a short form, one row for each booklet without the dates, for the Treasurer. The name of the treasurer at the top comes from the active record of the Treasurer page (Section 2.6.12). It has a Report No line to write by hand and the column Remarks, which shows the collector that holds the booklet.

Step 1. Click Consolidated Report of Accountability for Accountable Forms in the menu (1), then click Filter (2).

Figure 5.6. Step 1. The report when it opens. 1 The menu item · 2 Filter. The names are blurred.

Step 2. Its filter has only the Year. Type the year (it starts as the present year) and click Filter.

Figure 5.7. Step 2. The filter window. 1 Year · 2 Filter.

Step 3. The report shows all the booklets of the year. Remarks shows the collector of each booklet.

Figure 5.8. Step 3. The report after Filter. 1 Filter · 2 Print · 3 Remarks: the collector of each booklet. The names are blurred.

At the end of the report is the line Certified Correct with the name of the Municipal Treasurer and a line for the date.

Figure 5.9. The end of the report. 1 Certified Correct, the Municipal Treasurer and the date. The names are blurred.

Print. Click Print. TIS opens the print window of the browser with the report as it is on the page. Choose the printer, or Save as PDF, and print.

5.3 Abstract of Collection

The Abstract of Collection lists the receipts that were issued, with the payee, the account and the amount. When it opens it is empty, with the columns OR Date, OR No., Gender, Payee, Account Code, Particulars and Amount and a Total. The example uses October 21, 2026.

Step 1. Click Abstract of Collection in the menu (1), then click Filter (2).

Figure 5.10. Step 1. The empty report. 1 The menu item · 2 Filter. The name and email are blurred.

Step 2. In Filter To Display tick the check box of each way of filtering that you want:

  • Filter By Date: from Date & Time From to Date & Time To. Choose October 21, 2026 in both boxes.
  • Filter By Collector: one collector.
  • Filter By Fund: one fund code. Filter By OR Template: one collection type (the list of Section 2.7).

The Filter button of the window stays gray until you tick at least one. When a box that is needed is empty, Collector required! or Fund required! appears. Click Filter (3).

Figure 5.11. Step 2. The filter window with Filter By Date ticked. 1 The check box · 2 The dates · 3 Filter. The payee names are blurred.

Step 3. The report shows the period, For the period of 21/10/2026 to 21/10/2026, and is grouped by form. Under each FORM (for example AF#51) the receipts are listed with their date, number, payee, account and amount. The buttons at the top are Filter, Print and Export To Excel.

Figure 5.12. Step 3. The abstract after Filter. 1 Filter · 2 Print · 3 Export To Excel · 4 The columns. The payee names are blurred.

At the end of each form TIS adds a Sub Total for the month and a Sub Total for the form, and then the Total of the report.

Figure 5.13. The end of the report. 1 The sub totals and the Total.

Print. Click Print to print the abstract with the browser print window. Click Export To Excel to save it as a spreadsheet file.

5.4 Report of Collection and Deposit (RCD)

This report shows one RCD made in RCD Processing. When it opens it is empty: RCD No., Liq Officer, Collector, RCD Date, Amount and Ref No. have no value, and the table has the columns OR Date, OR No., Payor, Particulars, Acct Code and Amount with a Grand Total.

Figure 5.14. The RCD report when it opens. 1 Search · 2 Import · 3 The details of the RCD · 4 The table.

5.4.1 Search, Retrieve and Print

Follow these steps. The example uses the date October 21, 2026.

Step 1. Click Search. The window Filter To Display opens. Each way of finding an RCD has a check box at its left: Collector, Year, Month and, after the word OR, a single Date. The boxes are gray and the Filter button is gray until you tick one.

Figure 5.15. Step 1. Click Search (1). The filter window opens. 1 Search · 2 The check boxes · 3 Filter (gray until you tick a box).

Step 2. Tick the check box of the way that you want, here Date. Its box becomes active and the Filter button turns blue.

Figure 5.16. Step 2. The Date check box is ticked. 1 The check box · 2 The Date box, now active.

Step 3. Choose the date. Click the Date box. A calendar opens. Click the day, here the 21, then click OK. For Collector, Year or Month choose from the list or type in the box.

Figure 5.17. Step 3. The calendar. 1 The day 21 · 2 OK.

Step 4. Click Filter (highlighted). The box now reads October 21st.

Figure 5.18. Step 4. The date is October 21st. 1 The Date box · 2 Click Filter.

A list RCD No. List opens with the RCD numbers of that day and their amounts. Date from and date to are required and Date from cannot be after date to appear when the dates of a filter are wrong.

Step 5. Click Retrieve on the RCD that you want.

Figure 5.19. Step 5. The RCD No. List for October 21: two RCDs. 1 The list · 2 Retrieve.

The page shows the RCD: its number, the liquidating officer, the collector, the RCD date and the amount, and the table of all the receipts of the RCD, grouped by form, with a Grand Total.

Figure 5.20. The RCD after Retrieve. 1 Print · 2 The details of the RCD · 3 The receipts. The payors and the officer names are blurred.

Step 6. Click Print (box 1 of the figure above). A print page opens, the Collector's Report of Collections and Deposits of the Office of the Treasurer. It shows the fund, the accountable officer, the date and the RCD No., A. Collections (the forms with quantity, start and end numbers, amount and total), B. Remittances/Deposits and C. Accountability for Accountable Forms. Click its own Print button (box 1 below) to send it to the printer.

Figure 5.21. The print page of the RCD. 1 Print · 2 A. Collections · 3 B. Remittances/Deposits. The officer names are blurred.

5.4.2 Import RPT

Real property tax receipts are made in another system. Import brings them into the RCD. In the window Import RPT, choose the Date From, the Date To and Encoded By, then click Filter. The Encoded By list shows only the RPT users that are assigned to your user (Section 1.2.5). TIS then lists the RPT receipts of that person (From API RPT OR List) so that you can import them. The messages are Encoded by is required, No rpt permit OR records found and No OR records to import.

Caution. Importing saves the RPT receipts into the RCD. Filter only shows them first. The import itself was not pressed while this manual was prepared; it is described from the code.
Figure 5.22. The window Import RPT. 1 Date From and Date To · 2 Encoded By (the RPT users assigned to you) · 3 Filter.

5.5 Report of Collection by Liquidating Officer (RCLO)

This report shows one RCLO made in RCLO Processing, and records the deposits of its money. When it opens it is empty: RCLO No., RCLO Date, Liq Officer, Doc Date and Amount have no value. It has three parts: the table of the RCDs of the RCLO, the checks with a Breakdown box, and the table of deposits.

Figure 5.23. The RCLO report when it opens. 1 Search · 2 The details of the RCLO · 3 The RCDs · 4 The Breakdown · 5 The deposits.

5.5.1 Search, Retrieve and Print

The steps are the same as for the RCD (Section 5.4.1). The example uses October 21, 2026.

Step 1. Click Search. In Filter To Display the check boxes are Liquidation Officer, Year, Month and, after OR, a single Date. Filter is gray until you tick one.

Figure 5.24. Step 1. Click Search (1). 1 Search · 2 The check boxes · 3 Filter (gray until you tick a box).

Step 2. Tick Date, then click the Date box and choose the day in the calendar (here the 21), and click OK.

Figure 5.25. Step 2. The calendar. 1 The day 21 · 2 OK.

Step 3. Click Filter. The Date box reads October 21st.

Figure 5.26. Step 3. Date ticked and set to October 21st. 1 The Date check box · 2 Click Filter.

The RCLO No. List opens with the RCLO numbers of that day and their amounts.

Step 4. Click Retrieve on the RCLO that you want.

Figure 5.27. Step 4. The RCLO No. List for October 21: one RCLO, 9,388.40. 1 The list · 2 Retrieve.

The page shows the RCLO. The table lists the collector, the RCD date, the RCD No, and for each form its Qty, Start No, End No and Amount, with a Sub Total and a Grand Total. In the example, the RCLO 100(01) GENERAL FUND10212026000014 has one RCD with 8 receipts (1,070.00) and 18 receipts (8,318.40), a total of 9,388.40. The Breakdown shows the cash (Current) and the checks.

Figure 5.28. The RCLO after Retrieve. 1 Print · 2 The RCDs and the totals · 3 The Breakdown and Add Deposit. The collector and officer names are blurred.

Step 5. Click Print (box 1 above). A print page opens with A. Collections (the receipts with date issued, serial number, payor, particulars and amount), B. Remittances/Deposits (the accountable officer or bank, the reference and the amount) and C. Accountability for Accountable Forms. Click its own Print button (box 1 below) to send it to the printer.

Figure 5.29. The print page of the RCLO. 1 Print · 2 A. Collections · 3 B. Remittances/Deposits. The officer names and payors are blurred.

5.5.2 Breakdown and deposits

The Breakdown box adds up the money of the RCLO: Current (cash), Check (the checks of the receipts, listed on the left with their number, date, bank and amount) and the Grand Total. The money has to be deposited. Click Add Deposit and fill in the window:

  • Bank / Accountable Officer: where the money goes (a bank of the Bank Depository page, or an officer).
  • Method: Cash or Check.
  • Validation No / O.R / Deposit Slip: the number of the deposit slip or receipt.
  • Deposit Date (starts as today) and Amount.

Click Create. RCLO deposits created successfully appears and the deposit shows in the table of deposits at the bottom (bank or officer, cash, validation number, date and amount). Add Deposit is gray until an RCLO is on the page.

Figure 5.30. The window Add Deposit. 1 The boxes of the deposit · 2 Create. The collector and officer names are blurred.
Printing. The Print buttons of the RCD and RCLO reports open the printed forms of these reports (with the treasurer's certification). They were not opened while this manual was prepared.
