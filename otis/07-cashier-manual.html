TIS User Manual

The Cashier

The Cashier is the person who receives payments at the treasury. This manual follows the sidebar of the Cashier, item by item: Collection (seven pages), Process (two pages) and Reports (five pages). For each page it shows what the page is for, what each button opens, and what you see after you filter or retrieve. Every figure was taken with a Cashier account.

Reading the colored boxes. Messages of TIS are shown in boxes: gray for a question or notice, green for success, red for an error and yellow for a warning. In the figures, red boxes with numbers point to the parts that the text explains, and names of people are blurred.

1. The Cashier workspace

When a user with the role Cashier signs in, the header shows Cashier and the sidebar has three groups, then Exit. Click the menu icon (three lines) at the top left to open the sidebar. The Admin menus (Transaction, Referential and Admin) do not appear.

Figure 1. The sidebar of the Cashier, with Collection open. 1 Collection · 2 The seven Collection pages · 3 Process · 4 Reports. The name and email are blurred.

Pages that list receipts, RCDs or RCLOs only read the data when you filter or retrieve. Pressing Print prints a page as it is. Nothing changes the records until you press a button such as Create, Process, Remove, Import or Add Deposit, which this manual only describes.

2. Collection

The Collection group makes the official receipts. Six of its pages have the same layout, the seventh (Online Request) lists requests made online.

The process of every receipt page (in general)

All six receipt pages follow the same process. The two examples in this manual, Issuance of Official Receipt (details typed by the cashier) and Building Permit (details retrieved from an application), show it step by step. Business Permit(OR), Community Tax Certificate - Ind, Community Tax Certificate - Corp and Burial Permit and Fee Receipt work the same way.

  1. Check the number. Type the OR number (or CTC number) from your booklet and click Check. TIS shows the officer, collector and form type of the booklet. If the number is not available, a message appears (for example that it is already used, not found, or not of an officer assigned to you).
  2. Get the details. Either type them (payor, gender, address, collection type) or retrieve them from the record of the permit with Add Details.
  3. Look at the Template List. It lists the accounts that are paid, their amounts and the total. Add or change lines if the page allows it. Add checks in the Check List.
  4. Click Save (or Ctrl+S). A green message confirms it.
  5. Print. Print the receipt, or find it later with Select OR To Print.

2.1 Issuance of Official Receipt(OR)

Makes a receipt for any payment that has no page of its own. The page has two parts: the Template List (the accounts being paid, with their amounts) and the Check List (the checks that the payor gave). Add Template and Add Check are gray until a receipt number has been checked.

The steps below follow one receipt from the number to Save. To look at a receipt that is already made, click Select OR To Print (box 1 of the next figure).

Figure 2. The page when it opens. 1 Select OR To Print · 2 OR No. · 3 Check · 4 Template List · 5 Check List.

The button Select OR To Print. A window opens with the receipts of the day: the date, the OR number and the payor. Type in the search box (box 1) to find an OR number or a payor. Click Select (box 3) on the receipt that you want.

Figure 3. The window Select OR to print. 1 Search OR No. or payor · 2 The list of receipts · 3 Select. The payors are blurred.

The receipt opens in a small window with its number, date, payor, the accounts and amounts, the total in words and the checks. Click Print (box 2) to print it.

Figure 4. The receipt that was selected. 1 The receipt · 2 Print. The payor is blurred.

Making a receipt, step by step

Step 1. Check the number. Type the receipt number from your booklet in OR No. and click Check (or press Ctrl+Enter). The form opens. The box at the right shows the Liq Officer, the Collector and the Form Type of the booklet, and the RCD No. and RCLO No. stay empty until the receipt is processed. The Document Date and OR Date start as today.

Step 2. Fill in the payor. Type the Payor, choose the Gender and check the Address. Business Type, Business Info and Remarks can stay N/A. A box that is left empty shows Required under it when you save.

Figure 5. The form after Check. 1 OR No. and Check · 2 Payor · 3 Gender · 4 Collection Type.

Step 3. Choose the Collection Type. Open the Collection Type list and choose the payment, for example Aggregate Landholdings. The list can be searched by typing. The Fund Code is filled in for you.

Figure 6. The Collection Type list. 1 The collection types (the OR templates).

TIS asks Do you want to add the template? Click Yes to put the lines of that collection type in the Template List, or No to stop.

Figure 7. The question. 1 The question · 2 Yes.

A green message Adding template to table success appears and the Template List shows the lines: the account code, the particulars and the amount, with the total below.

Figure 8. The template added. 1 Collection Type · 2 The message · 3 The Template List and its total.

Step 4. Add another collection type. If the payor pays for something else, click Add Template. In Select Additional Collection Type choose the type and answer Yes to the question. Its lines are added under the others and the total grows.

Figure 9. The button and the window. 1 Add Template · 2 Select Additional Collection Type.
Figure 10. After the second template. 1 The message · 2 The Template List, now with three lines and a total of 110.00 · 3 Add Check.

Step 5. Change a line (if needed). Click the pencil icon of a line. The window Edit shows the Particulars and the Amount. Change them and click Update, or click Close to leave without a change.

Figure 11. The window Edit. 1 Particulars, Amount, Close and Update · 2 The pencil icon of the line.

Step 6. Checks. If the payor pays by check, click Add Check and fill in the check number, date, bank and amount. The checks appear in the Check List.

Step 7. Click Save. The buttons at the top right of the form are Select OR To Print/Copy, Save and Erase.

Figure 12. The buttons of the form. 1 Select OR To Print/Copy · 2 Save · 3 Erase.

What Save does. Click Save (or press Ctrl+S). Save is gray until the form has at least one line in the Template List. TIS then records the receipt under the OR number, with the payor, gender, address, collection type, the dates and the total amount (split between special accounts and the other accounts). The green message Issuance details updated successfully appears and the receipt is shown again as saved, with its lines and checks. After a moment the receipt opens for printing, and then TIS asks Next OR? Choose Yes to continue with the next number of the booklet. A saved receipt can later be found with Select OR To Print/Copy. Erase cancels a receipt that was saved, and shows Issuance OR cancelled successfully.

2.2 Business Permit(OR)

Makes the receipt of a business permit. The business and its fees come from the assessment of the business. It has the same layout as the page above: OR No., Check, Template List and Check List, and Select OR To Print.

Figure 13. The page when it opens. 1 Select OR To Print · 2 OR No. · 3 Check · 4 Template List.

What a cashier sees on Check. A receipt can be handled only when its booklet belongs to an officer that is assigned to your user. If you type a number of another officer, TIS shows a red message. Click OK and ask the Admin to assign the officer to your user.

Figure 14. Check on a number that is not of an assigned officer. 1 OR No. · 2 Check · 3 The message · 4 OK.

The message reads You are not allowed to proccess this personnel.

2.3 Building Permit

Makes the receipt of a building permit from the application of the permit. This is the sample for the pages that take their data from another office: the payor and the fees are not typed, they are retrieved from the application. Business Permit(OR) and the other Collection pages follow the same process (check the number, retrieve or fill in the details, look at the Template List, click Save, print). Only the source of the details differs.

Step 1. Check the number. Type the OR number and click Check. The form opens with the officer, collector and form type of the booklet. The Payor is empty, Save is gray and the Template List shows No record found. Click Add Details.

Figure 15. The form after Check. 1 OR No. and Check · 2 Add Details · 3 The empty Template List. The officers are blurred.

Step 2. Find the application. The window Filter To Display offers two ways: tick Search for Application No. and type the number, or tick Search for Owner and type the name (the owner is required). Click Filter.

Figure 16. The window Filter To Display. 1 Search for Application No. · 2 Search for Owner · 3 Filter.

The list shows each application with its number and the owner. Use the search box to narrow it. Click Retrieve on the application of the payor.

Figure 17. The list of applications. 1 Search application no./owner · 2 Retrieve. The owner is blurred.

Step 3. Look at the receipt. The Payor is filled in and the Template List shows the fees of the permit with their account codes and the total, here 5,808.50 and 468.00 for 6,276.50. Save is now blue. If the payor pays by check, use Add Check as in 2.1.

Figure 18. The form after Retrieve. 1 Payor · 2 The fees of the permit and the total · 3 Save. The names are blurred.

Step 4. Click Save (or press Ctrl+S).

Figure 19. The buttons of the page. 1 Select OR To Print · 2 Save.

The green message Building permit created successfully appears. The button Save is replaced by Print OR, and the lines of the Template List now show the particulars of the accounts (for example Accessory Structure Fees and Electrical Fees).

Figure 20. After Save. 1 The message · 2 Print OR · 3 The saved fees. The names are blurred.

Step 5. Print. Click Print OR to print the receipt on the official form: the date, the OR number, the fund, the payor, the lines with their account codes and amounts, the total in figures and in words, and the name of the treasurer. To print an older receipt, use Select OR To Print.

Figure 21. The printed receipt. The payor and the treasurer are blurred.

2.7 Online Request

Lists the payments that people requested online, so that the cashier can find a request and set it as paid. The list shows the number, the payor and the Service Type (for example burial). Use the search box (box 1) to find a payor, Scan QR (box 2) to read the QR code of a request with the camera, or Upload QR (box 3) to read it from a picture file. The eye icon in the Action column opens the request.

Figure 25. The page when it opens. 1 Search payor · 2 Scan QR · 3 Upload QR · 4 The requests. The example is a test request.

The window Request Details shows the Request Information (payee, deceased, cemetery, dates and cause) and the Breakdown: the accounts to pay with their amounts. The pencil icon of each line changes that line.

Figure 26. The window Request Details. 1 Request Information · 2 Breakdown.

3. Process

Process closes the collection. The RCD gathers the receipts of one collector, and the RCLO gathers the RCDs of one fund. Both pages have two icons at the top right: the folder with a plus (to process) and the magnifier (to list what was processed).

3.1 Report of Collection and Deposits(RCD) Processing

The table shows the collector, the receipts Issued and Sold (quantity, from and to), the Collected Amount, the booklet Suffix, Fromno, Tono and the Balance. It is empty until you retrieve.

Figure 27. The page when it opens. 1 Process (folder icon) · 2 The list of RCDs (magnifier) · 3 The table of the collector.

The magnifier opens the RCD List: the date of each RCD, its number and the RCLO that holds it. Use the search box (box 1) to find an RCD number. The Remove button of a line is blue only for an RCD that is not yet in an RCLO.

Figure 28. The window RCD List. 1 Search RCD No. · 2 RCD date, RCD number and RCLO number.

The folder icon opens PROCESS RCD DIALOG. Choose the Liq Officer, the Collector (only the officers assigned to your user are offered), the Fund Code and the Form Code, then the From OR Date, To OR Date and the RCD Date. Click Retrieve to see the receipts of that choice. Process gives the RCD its number. Both buttons are gray until the lists are chosen.

Figure 29. The window PROCESS RCD DIALOG. 1 Liq Officer · 2 Collector · 3 Fund Code · 4 Form Code · 5 The dates · 6 RCD No. · 7 Retrieve · 8 Process.

3.2 Report of Collection by Liquidating Officer(RCLO) Processing

The table lists the RCDs that go into the RCLO: RCD No., Liquidating Officer, Collector, Money Value and RCD Date.

Figure 30. The page when it opens. 1 Process (folder icon) · 2 The list of RCLOs (magnifier) · 3 The table of RCDs.

The folder icon opens PROCESS RCLO DIALOG: choose the Fund Code, the From RCD Date and To RCD Date, and the RCLO Date. Retrieve shows the RCDs of the period. Process gives them the RCLO number.

4. Reports

The Reports group has five reports. The pages only show data. Each one has a Filter or Search button to choose what to show, and Print to print the report on the page. Printing opens the print window of the browser.

4.1 Accountable Forms: Report of Accountability for Accountable Forms

Shows what happened to each booklet of receipts during a year.

Step 1. The page opens with the empty report. Click Filter (box 1).

Figure 33. The report when it opens. 1 Filter · 2 Print · 3 The columns of the report.

Step 2. In Filter To Display the Year starts as the present year. Tick Filter By Collector to choose one collector, or leave it empty to show all. Click Filter.

Figure 34. The filter window. 1 Year · 2 Filter By Collector and the Collector list · 3 Filter.

Step 3. Each row is one booklet: its form code, date, the beginning balance, what was issued and the ending balance. Click Print to print it.

Figure 35. The report after Filter. 1 Filter · 2 Print · 3 The columns · 4 One row for each booklet.

4.2 Accountable Forms: Consolidated Report of Accountability for Accountable Forms

The same report in a short form, for the Treasurer, with a Report No line to write by hand and a Remarks column with the collector who holds each booklet. The filter has only the Year.

Figure 36. The report when it opens. 1 Filter · 2 Print · 3 The columns. The name of the treasurer is blurred.
Figure 37. The filter window. 1 Year · 2 Filter.
Figure 38. The report after Filter. 1 Filter · 2 Print · 3 Remarks: the collector of each booklet. The names are blurred.

4.3 Collection: Abstract of Collection

Lists the receipts that were issued, with the payee, the account and the amount, grouped by form and month, with sub totals and a total. The buttons are Filter, Print and Export To Excel (saves the report as a spreadsheet).

Figure 39. The report when it opens. 1 Filter · 2 Print · 3 Export To Excel · 4 The columns.

Click Filter. Tick the check box of each way of filtering: Filter By Date (from and to), Filter By Collector, Filter By Fund and Filter By OR Template. A list is active only after its box is ticked. Filter stays gray until you tick at least one box. If a ticked list is empty, Collector required! or Fund required! appears.

Figure 40. The filter window with Filter By Date ticked. 1 Date · 2 Collector · 3 Fund · 4 OR Template · 5 Filter.

The report then shows the period, for example For the period of 27/10/2026 to 27/10/2026, and the receipts.

Figure 41. The abstract after Filter. 1 Filter · 2 Print · 3 Export To Excel · 4 The columns. The payee names are blurred.

Shows one RCLO made in RCLO Processing and records the deposits of its money. When it opens it is empty. It has the table of the RCDs, a list of checks with a Breakdown box and the table of deposits.

Step 1. Click Search. The check boxes are Liquidation Officer, Year, Month and, after OR, a single Date.

Figure 49. The filter window. 1 Liquidation Officer · 2 Year · 3 Month · 4 Date · 5 Filter.

Step 3. Click Retrieve on the RCLO that you want.

The page shows the RCLO: the collector, the RCD date, the RCD number and, for each form, its quantity, start number, end number and amount, with a Sub Total and a Grand Total. The Breakdown adds up the cash (Current) and the Check.

Add Deposit (gray until an RCLO is on the page) opens a window: the Bank / Accountable Officer, the Method (cash or check), the Validation No / O.R / Deposit Slip, the Deposit Date and the Amount. Figure 52. The window Add Deposit. 1 Bank / Accountable Officer and Method · 2 Validation No · 3 Deposit Date · 4 Amount · 5 Create.

Print. The button Print opens the Report of Collections and Deposits with the collections (date issued, serial numbers, payor, particulars, amount), the remittances and deposits, and the accountability for accountable forms. Click its own Print button to print it.

Figure 53. The print page. 1 Print · 2 The accountable officer and the RD number · 3 A. Collections · 4 B. Remittances/Deposits. The names are blurred.

5. What limits a cashier

  • Assigned officers. A receipt can be handled only when its booklet belongs to an officer assigned to your user. Otherwise TIS shows You are not allowed to proccess this personnel.. The Collector list of RCD Processing offers only the assigned officers.
  • Assigned RPT users. Import RPT offers only the RPT users assigned to your user.
  • No set-up pages. Funds, accounts, collection types, officers, booklets and the OR templates are kept by the Admin. If one is missing, ask the Admin.
  • Erase. The Erase button of the Business Permit page is shown only to the Admin.
