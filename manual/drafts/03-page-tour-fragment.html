Figure 2.22. The account menu. Your name is hidden in this figure.
OptionUse it to

LogoutSign out of eBPLS. Always log out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

Settings

Click the settings icon (the gear) in the top bar to open the Settings panel. These options change how the pages are displayed on your device only. They do not change any permit or business record.

Figure 2.23. The Settings panel. Your name is hidden in this figure.
SettingWhat it does
RTLSwitches the page layout to right-to-left. Leave it off for normal left-to-right reading.
Responsive font sizesAdjusts the size of the text to fit your screen. It is on by default.
ThemeChanges the color theme of the pages. The default is Light.
Save SettingsClick to apply the settings you chose.
