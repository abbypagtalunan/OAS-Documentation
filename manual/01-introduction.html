1.1 About OAS Unified Online Services

OAS Unified Online Services brings the municipality's online systems together under one website. Residents, business owners, property owners and builders can find the service they need in one place, and each service has its own dedicated system. Using these systems means faster processing, clearer information on the status of an application, and more efficient service from the local government.

1.2 Accessing the OAS website

  1. Open a web browser (for example Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Safari).
  2. Go to https://oas.lguapp.com.
  3. The OAS home page opens. Scroll to the What We Offer section to see the LGU Online Applications.
  4. Click the card for the service you need. Each card opens that system in the same browser window.

1.3 LGU Online Applications

The home page lists five online applications. Choose the one that matches your need.

ApplicationWho it is for and what it doesAddress
New/Register Business Permit
(BPLS)		 Business owners. The Business Permit and Licensing System simplifies the application and renewal of business permits, with faster processing and real-time application status. ebpls.oas.lguapp.com
MediLink Residents. The Health & Appointment System lets you schedule appointments with the Municipal Health Office, receive reminders and access health services with less waiting time. medilink.oas.lguapp.com
RPT Billing
(RPTIS)		 Property owners. The Real Property Tax Information System provides property records, tax assessments and billing information. rptpayment.oas.lguapp.com
Other Services (Treasury)
(TIS)		 Anyone paying the municipality. The Treasury Information System handles secure payment processing, official receipt generation, real-time transaction posting and financial reporting. tis.oas.lguapp.com
Building Permit & Certificate of Occupancy
(BPCOS)		 Builders and developers. The system covers permit applications, compliance verification, inspections and occupancy certification, in line with the National Building Code. bpcos.oas.lguapp.com
Which system does this manual cover in detail? The chapters that follow focus on the Business Permit and Licensing System (BPLS): how applicants apply and renew, and how municipal staff process, assess, approve and release the Mayor's Permit.
