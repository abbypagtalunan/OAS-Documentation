Section 1

Introduction

This manual explains how to use the Online Business Permit and Licensing System (eBPLS) of the Municipality of Oas, Albay, for the business owners who apply and for the municipal staff who process the applications.

In this section
  • 1.1 About eBPLS
  • 1.2 Accessing eBPLS
  • 1.3 How this manual is organized

1.1 About eBPLS

eBPLS is the online Business Permit and Licensing System of the Municipality of Oas, one of the online applications of the OAS website. It simplifies the application and renewal of business permits, with faster processing, clearer information on the status of an application and more efficient service from the local government. Business owners apply online for a new business, a renewal, a closure or a transfer. The municipal staff then process, assess, approve and release the Mayor's Permit in the same system.

1.2 Accessing eBPLS

  1. Open a web browser (for example Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Safari).
  2. Go to https://oas.lguapp.com.
  3. The OAS home page opens. Scroll to the What We Offer section to see the LGU Online Applications.
  4. Click the New/Register Business Permit card. It opens eBPLS (ebpls.oas.lguapp.com) in the same browser window.
