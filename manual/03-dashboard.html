Section 3

The Dashboard

After you sign in, eBPLS opens the Dashboard, which is the Business Holdings page. This section describes what you see on it and how to use its menus.

3.1 Overview

The Dashboard has three areas:

  • Top bar (blue, across the top): chat, notifications, settings and your account menu. See Section 3.2.
  • Sidebar (left side): links to the Dashboard, Payment History and other applications. See Section 3.3.
  • Main content: the Business Holdings page, where your businesses are listed. See Section 3.4.
Figure 3.1. The Dashboard (Business Holdings page) of a new account.
No business holding record yet. When an account has no business holding record, the Business Holdings page appears as shown in the figure above: the table lists only its column headings, and the page count at the bottom reads 0-0 of 0. This is the normal appearance of a newly created account and does not indicate an error.

3.2 Top bar

Figure 3.2. The top bar, with an arrow and a label for each icon: Chat, Notifications, Settings and Account.

Notifications (bell icon)See your notifications, such as updates on your requests.
Settings (gear icon)Open the display settings (see Settings below).
Account (your profile picture and name)Open the account menu with Account and Logout (see Account menu below).

Account menu

Click your name at the top right to open the account menu.

Figure 3.3. The account menu.
  • Account: Open your account page to view and update your profile details.
  • Logout: Sign out of eBPLS. Always log out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

Settings

Click the settings icon (the gear) in the top bar to open the Settings panel. These options change how the pages are displayed on your device only. They do not change any permit or business record.

Figure 3.4. The Settings panel.
  • RTL: Switches the page layout to right-to-left. Leave it off for normal left-to-right reading.
  • Responsive font sizes: Adjusts the size of the text to fit your screen. It is on by default.
  • Theme: Changes the color theme of the pages. The default is Light.
  • Save Settings: Click to apply the settings you chose.

3.4 Business Holdings

The Business Holdings page lists the businesses under your account. The buttons at the top right and the tools above the table are:

ItemUse it to
Watch Guide VideoWatch the guide video (see below).
Add New CompanyRegister a new business under your account (see Section 4).
Business RegistrationShows another video demo.
Search BusinessFind a business in your list by typing its name.

Add New Company is explained in Section 4.

Watch Guide Video

Click Watch Guide Video to open the Guide Video window. It plays a short tutorial on using the system to request an assessment. The window also opens by itself the first time you visit this page in a browser.

Figure 3.5. The Guide Video window.
  • Use the video controls to play, pause and adjust the sound.
  • Click the X at the top right of the window to close it. Once you close it, it does not open by itself again in that browser, but you can always open it with Watch Guide Video.

3.5 Payment History

Click Payment History, under Reports in the sidebar, to open the Payment History List. It lists the payments recorded for your businesses.

Figure 3.6. The Payment History List with no payment record yet.
  • OR Date: The date of the official receipt.
  • Business Name: The business the payment belongs to.
  • OR Number: The official receipt number.
  • Amount: The amount paid.

Click the three-dot icon (More options) at the top right of the list and choose Export Pdf to download the list as a PDF file. Use Rows per page and the arrows at the bottom to move through the list.

No payment record yet. When no payment has been recorded for your businesses, the list shows only its column headings and the page count reads 0-0 of 0. This is normal for an account that has not made a payment.
