Section 8

Requesting a Permit: Transfer

A Transfer request is for a business that is being transferred. The steps are similar to a new business request (Section 5), with the differences described here.

8.1 Open the request form

Open the assessment list and click Request, as described in Section 5.1. On the Request Form, click Transaction Type and choose Transfer.

8.2 Fill in the details

For a transfer, the form does not ask for Capitalization or Gross Income. After Transaction Type, it goes straight to No of Employees.

Figure 8.1. The Request Form with Transfer selected. There is no Capitalization or Gross Income field.
  • Business Name: the name of your business. It is filled in for you and cannot be changed.
  • Business Category and Business Type (required): filled in from the business record. Change them only if they are wrong.
  • Transaction Year: the year of the request.
  • Transaction Type (required): choose Transfer.
  • No of Employees: the number of employees.
  • Total Floor Area (required): the floor area of the business premises. It must be at least 1.
  • No. of Storey (required): the number of storeys. It must be at least 1.
  • Contact No: the contact number of the business.
  • Owner Name: the name of the business owner.
  • Authorized Representative (required): the person authorized to transact for the business.

8.3 Choose the payment mode

For a Transfer, you can choose Annual, Semi-Annual or Quarterly. If you choose Semi-Annual or Quarterly, choose the period as described in Section 6.3.

8.4 Attach the requirements

For a Transfer, the Attach Requirements section says No requirements needed. You have nothing to attach.

Figure 8.2. The bottom of the Request Form for a Transfer. The Attach Requirements section says "No requirements needed."

8.5 Send the request

Click Request to send the request, or Cancel to close the form without sending. If a field needs correction, see Section 5.3.
