Section 4

Adding a New Business

Before you can request a permit, your business must be listed under your account. This section shows how to add a new business using Add New Company.

4.1 Before you begin

Have the following information ready:

  • The business name, franchise name, category and type, and a description of the business.
  • The complete business address, including the barangay.
  • The business email address.
  • The DTI registration number and the date it was issued.
  • The owner's name, address and gender.
  • The number of employees and the date the business started.
Required fields. You must complete the following fields before the business can be saved. Fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the form are required; Business Category, Owner Type, Barangay and Business Line Desc. are also required even though they have no asterisk.
  • General Information: Business Name, Franchise Name, Business Category, Business Type, Owner Type, Business Line Desc., Barangay, Complete Address, Company Email.
  • Registration: DTI No., DTI Date Issued.
  • Registered Owner: Owner, Owner Address, Owner Gender, No. of Employee (at least 1), Date Start.
  • Miscellaneous: none.
Text you type in most fields is converted to capital letters automatically.

4.2 Fill out the new business form

  1. On the Dashboard, click Add New Company (see Section 3.4).
Figure 4.1. Click Add New Company (highlighted) on the Business Holdings page.
  1. The New Business page opens. The path at the top of the page reads Dashboard, Management, Companies, Create.
  2. Fill in the four sections of the form, one after the other, as described in 4.2.1 to 4.2.4. Their names appear in the left column, highlighted in red in the figures.
  • General Information: the business name, category, type, address and contact details.
  • Registration: the DTI registration and other identification numbers.
  • Registered Owner: the owner and representative details, the business size and the key dates.
  • Miscellaneous: rental and other business-specific details.

4.2.1 General Information

This is the first section of the form.

Figure 4.2. The New Business page, showing the General Information section.
  • Business Name (required): the registered name of the business.
  • Franchise Name (required): the franchise name of the business.
  • Business Category (required): choose the category from the list. A category may already be selected when the form opens; change it to match your business.
  • Business Type (required): choose the type from the list. The list depends on the category you chose.
  • Owner Type (required): choose Corporation, Cooperative, Foundation, Partnership or Sole Proprietor.
  • Business Line Desc. (required): describe what the business does.
  • Street, Lot/Block No. and Street Code: the street, lot or block number and street code of the business address. Street Code starts at 0.
  • Barangay (required): choose the barangay from the list.
  • Province and Municipality: pre-filled with ALBAY and OAS. Change them only if the business is elsewhere.
  • Complete Address (required): the full address of the business.
  • Telephone No. and Fax No.: contact numbers of the business.
  • Company Email (required): the business email address. It must be a valid email.
  • Latitude and Longitude: the map coordinates of the business location.
Figure 4.3. The rest of the General Information section (Complete Address to Longitude), followed by the Registration section.

4.2.2 Registration

The Registration section follows General Information (see the lower part of Figure 4.3).

  • DTI No. (required): the DTI registration number.
  • DTI Date Issued (required): the date the DTI registration was issued. Choose it from the calendar.
  • TIN, PhilHealth, SSS and HDMF: the business identification numbers. SSS and HDMF accept up to 15 characters.
  • SEC Registration No. and SEC Date Issued: these two fields appear only when the Owner Type is not Sole Proprietor.

4.2.3 Registered Owner

Figure 4.4. The Registered Owner section.
Figure 4.5. The end of the Registered Owner section (signboard and dates), followed by the Miscellaneous section.
  • Owner (required): the full name of the business owner.
  • Owner Address (required): the owner's address.
  • Owner Gender (required): choose Male, Female or N/A.
  • Mobile No.: the owner's mobile number.
  • President and Treasurer: for organizations, the names of the president and the treasurer.
  • Representative, Representative Address and Representative Position: the person who represents the business, with address and position.
  • Capitalization: the capital of the business, in pesos.
  • No. of Employee (required): the number of employees. Enter at least 1.
  • Total Floor Area and No. of Storey: the floor area and the number of storeys of the business premises.
  • Using Signboard: choose Yes or No. If you choose Yes, an Area of Signboard field appears.
  • Date Start (required): the date the business started operating. Choose it from the calendar.
  • Date Applied, Date Renew, Date of Transfer and Date Closed: choose each date from the calendar. Leave them empty unless they apply.

4.2.4 Miscellaneous

Figure 4.6. The Miscellaneous section.
  • Rented: choose Yes if the business premises are rented. If you choose Yes, Since When, Monthly Rental and From Whom appear.
  • Office Type, Type Of Bank and Type Of Club: type-specific details. Leave them at 0 if they do not apply to your business.
  • No. of Heads If Farm: for farms, the number of heads. Leave at 0 if it does not apply.
  • Bedroom Type, No. of Bedrooms, No. Of Occupancy and With AC: for lodging businesses, the bedroom details. Leave them at 0 if they do not apply.
Figure 4.7. The end of the form, with the Cancel and Add Company buttons.

4.3 Saving the business

At the bottom of the form there are two buttons:

Figure 4.8. The Cancel and Add Company buttons.
  • Add Company: saves the business. A green message, Company Created, appears and you are taken back to the Business Holdings page, where the new business now appears in the list.
  • Cancel: closes the form without saving and returns to the Business Holdings page.

If a required field is missing

If a required field is empty when you click Add Company, the business is not saved. The empty field is outlined in red and a red message appears under it, as shown below.

Figure 4.9. Required fields left empty, outlined in red with a message under each.
Error: Business name is required and Franchise name is required (as in the figure). Fill in every field that shows a red message and click Add Company again. Scroll through all four sections, because a field with a message may be above or below the part of the form you are viewing.

The messages you may see are:

  • Business name is required: appears under Business Name.
  • Franchise name is required: appears under Franchise Name.
  • Business category is required and Business type is required: appear under Business Category and Business Type.
  • Owner type is required: appears under Owner Type.
  • Business line description is required: appears under Business Line Desc.
  • Barangay is required: appears under Barangay.
  • Business address is required: appears under Complete Address.
  • Company email is required and Invalid email address: appear under Company Email.
  • Owner is required, Owner address is required and Owner gender is required: appear under Owner, Owner Address and Owner Gender.
  • No. of employee is required: appears under No. of Employee.
  • Date start is required: appears under Date Start.

4.4 After the business is added

After you click Add Company, the system returns you to the Business Holdings page. The business you just added now appears in the list, and the page count at the bottom changes from 0-0 of 0 to 1-1 of 1.

Figure 4.10. The Business Holdings page showing the newly added business (highlighted in red).

The first column of the row, Assessment Request, has three icons:

  • Edit Business (pencil icon): opens the business record so you can review or change its details.
  • Step One - Display Assessment List (numbered 1 icon): opens the business's assessment list, where permit requests are made. A label with this name appears next to the icon while the business has no assessment request yet.
  • Location (pin icon): shows the business location.
If the numbered icon is greyed out, the business record is missing required details. Hover over the icon to read the message, then click Edit Business to complete the details.

4.5 Viewing the business details

The business you added appears on the Business Holdings page. The table has four tabs: General Info, Registration, Registered Owner and Miscellaneous. Click a tab to see a different set of details for each business. The Assessment Request and Business columns stay on every tab. If the table is wider than your screen, scroll sideways to see all columns.

4.5.1 General Info tab

Figure 4.11. The General Info tab.
  • Assessment Request: the icons for the business (see Section 4.4).
  • Business: the business name.
  • Franchise Name: the franchise name of the business.
  • Line Of Business: what the business does.
  • Complete Address: the full address of the business.
  • Business Type Code: the business type, such as General Merchandise.
  • Business Code: the number the system assigned to the business.
  • Owner Type: the owner type, shown as a letter, such as S for Sole Proprietor.
  • Street, Barangay, Street Code and Lot/Block No: the parts of the business address.

4.5.2 Registration tab

Figure 4.12. The Registration tab.
  • SEC Registration No and SEC Date Issued: the SEC details. They are empty for a sole proprietor.
  • DTI Number and DTI Date Issued: the DTI registration number and the date it was issued.
  • TIN, PhilHealth, SSS and HDMF: the business identification numbers, when provided.

4.5.3 Registered Owner tab

Figure 4.13. The Registered Owner tab.
  • Owner, Address, Gender and Mobile: the details of the business owner. Gender is shown as a letter, such as F for Female.
  • President/GM, Treasurer, Representative and Representative Position: the officers and representative of the business, when provided.

4.5.4 Miscellaneous tab

Figure 4.14. The Miscellaneous tab.
  • Rented: Y if the business premises are rented.
  • Since when, Monthly Rental and From Whom: the rental details.
  • Type of Office, Type of Bank and Type of Club: type-specific details. They show 0 when they do not apply.
  • Bedroom Type, No. of Occupancy, No. of Bedrooms and With A/C: the details for lodging businesses. They show 0 when they do not apply.
