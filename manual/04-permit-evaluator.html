Section 4

Permit Evaluator: Reviewing New Requests

This section is for OAS staff who evaluate and assess permit requests. It shows how to sign in as the Permit Evaluator and find the requests waiting for your action.

4.1 Sign in as the Permit Evaluator

  1. Open the eBPLS sign-in page, as described in Section 2.2.
  2. Enter the Email Address and Password of the Permit Evaluator account. Your administrator gives you these.
  3. Click Log In.
Figure 4.1. The Sign in page. The email address and password are hidden in this figure.
Staff accounts use the same Sign in page as applicants. The system takes you to the screens for your role after you sign in. Do not use Create new account for a staff account.

4.2 The Assessment Request dashboard

After you sign in, the Assessment Request page opens. This is the dashboard of the Permit Evaluator. The name of your account, eBPLS Permit Evaluator, shows in the top bar and in the sidebar.

Figure 4.2. The Assessment Request dashboard of the Permit Evaluator, with the role highlighted in red.

The page has two lists, each with a red number that shows how many requests it holds:

  • New Request: requests that are waiting for you to review and approve (see Section 4.3).
  • Approved Request For Assessment: requests whose details you have already approved (see Section 4.6).

Click the name of a list, or the arrow at its right, to open or close it. At the top right, Watch Guide Video plays the guide video.

The sidebar

  • Request: returns to the Assessment Request dashboard.
  • Transactions: Assessment and Rollback Assessment.
  • Reports: Companies, Requirements Lists, Registered Businesses, Index Of Payments, Closed Businesses, Bus Category Per-Account, Business Summary and Summary of Collection per Barangay. The reports are described in Section 6.4.
  • Applications: Mail, Chat and Calendar.

4.3 New Request

The New Request list holds the requests that applicants have sent and that you have not yet reviewed. Click New Request to open it, as shown in Figure 4.2.

Each row is one request. The columns are:

  • Date: the date the applicant sent the request.
  • Business: the name of the business.
  • Period and Quarter: the payment mode of the request, for example Annually, or Quarterly with the quarter.
  • Type: the kind of request, for example New Business or Renewal.
  • Action: the paperclip icon shows the attachments and the requirements per office (see Section 4.4). The arrow icon opens the request so you can review it (see Section 4.5).

To find a request, type the name of the business in Search business, or choose the year in Year. Use Rows per page and the page arrows at the bottom to move through the list.

Figure 4.3. Type the name of the business in Search business. The list shows only the matching requests.
Figure 4.4. Click Year and choose the year of the requests. The list shows the years from 2018 up to the current year.

4.4 View the attachments and requirements of a request

To see what the applicant sent with a request, click the paperclip icon in the Action column of the request. The same icon is in the New Request list and in the Approved Request For Assessment list.

Figure 4.5. Click the paperclip icon (highlighted) to view the attachments and the requirements per office that the applicant submitted.

The Assessment Requirements page opens. It lists the offices that review the request: Assessor, Engineering, Fire, Peso, Mao, Menro, Mpdo, Rhu, Bplo and the barangay of the business. At the top of the page:

  • Search account: finds an office in the list.
  • Back: returns to the previous page.
  • Attachment On Request: shows the files the applicant attached when sending the request.
  • Watch Guide Video: plays the guide video.
Figure 4.6. The Assessment Requirements page, with one row for each office. The role is highlighted in red.

Check the requirements of an office

  1. Click the name of an office, or the arrow at its left, to open its requirements.
  2. The list shows each requirement as a row with its Description, its Status, the Date Submitted and an Action icon. The status SENT means the applicant has sent the file.
Figure 4.7. An office opened (Fire), showing its requirements as SENT with their dates.
  1. To find a requirement, type part of its name in Search requirements. In the example, typing "Occu" shows the Occupancy Permit.
Figure 4.8. Type part of a requirement name in Search requirements. The paperclip icon (highlighted) opens the files.
  1. Click the paperclip icon in the Action column of a requirement to view the files the applicant sent for it. The Uploaded Requirements Attachment window opens, with each file as a tile showing its file type and file name. Click the X at the top right to close the window.
Figure 4.9. The Uploaded Requirements Attachment window, showing the file sent for the requirement.

4.5 Review and approve a new request

Step 1. Open the request

In the New Request list, click the arrow icon in the Action column of the request.

Figure 4.10. Click the arrow icon (highlighted) to open the request.

The Assessment Request details page opens. The path at the top reads Dashboard, Details. Click Back at the top right to return to the list without making changes.

Step 2. Check the details

Check each field against what the applicant sent, and complete the ones that are empty:

Figure 4.11. The top of the details page, with the role highlighted in red.
  • Business Category and Business Type (required): filled in from the applicant's request. Change them only if they are wrong.
  • Transaction Date: the date of the request.
  • Transaction Type (required): the kind of request, for example New Business.
  • Business Year: the year of the request.
  • Owner Type (required): for example Sole Proprietor.
  • Capitalization: the capital of the business, in pesos. For a new business the form asks for the capitalization.
  • No. Of Employee (required): the number of employees.
  • Years Arrears: the number of years the business has arrears. Leave it empty if there are none.
  • CTC Number (required): the number of the community tax certificate (CTC) of the owner.
  • Date Of Issue: the date the CTC was issued.
  • Issued Address: the place where the CTC was issued.
  • Payment Mode (required): how the applicant will pay. It is filled in from the request.
Figure 4.12. The rest of the details page, ending with Approve and Cancel.
Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required. If a required field is empty, a red message appears under it when you click Approve.
Figure 4.13. A required field left empty. Here the CTC Number is empty and the message "CTC number is required" appears under it.
Error: CTC number is required appears under CTC Number when it is empty. Enter the number and click Approve again.

Step 3. Approve the request

At the bottom of the page, click Approve (highlighted) to approve the details. Click Cancel to leave the page without approving.

Figure 4.14. Click Approve (highlighted) at the bottom of the page.

When you click Approve, the system approves the details and then runs the assessment process by itself. You do not need to do anything else. Messages appear at the lower left: Sworn successfully approve., then Please wait while we run the assessment process..., and finally Assessment process completed successfully. You are then taken back to the dashboard.

Figure 4.15. The dashboard after approving. The message Assessment process completed successfully appears at the lower left, and the New Request count goes down.

The request leaves the New Request list, and the red number on New Request goes down by one. The request now appears in Approved Request For Assessment (see Section 4.6).

4.6 Approved Request For Assessment

After you approve the details of a new request, it moves to Approved Request For Assessment. Click the name of the list to open it. It has the same columns, search box and year filter as the New Request list.

Figure 4.16. The Approved Request For Assessment list. The request you approved appears in the list (highlighted). The business names of other applicants are hidden.

4.7 The For Assessment list

An approved request is also listed under Transactions. Click Assessment in the sidebar (highlighted in the figure) to open the Assessment page.

Figure 4.17. The Assessment page. Click Assessment in the sidebar (highlighted) to open it. The business names of other applicants are hidden.

The page has five lists. Click the name of a list to open it. The red number shows how many requests it holds.

  • For Assessment: approved requests whose assessment is waiting to be sent for approval. This is the list you work with next.
  • Assessed For Payment: requests that BPLO has approved and that are waiting for payment.
  • Paid Assessment Request: requests that have been paid.
  • Semi-Annual/Quarter Transaction: semi-annual and quarterly requests.
  • Closure Transaction: requests to close a business.

In For Assessment, each row shows the Date, Business, Period, Quarter, Type and the Assessment Status, which is PENDING until you send the assessment for approval. Use Search business, Filter Status and Year to find a request. In the Action column:

  • The document icon opens the files uploaded by the offices for the request (see below).
  • The paperclip icon shows the attachments and the requirements per office (see Section 4.4).
  • The arrow icon opens the assessment of the request (see Section 4.8).

View the files uploaded by an office

Click the document icon in the Action column of the request.

Figure 4.18. Click the document icon (highlighted) to see the files uploaded by the offices. The business names of other applicants are hidden.

The Files page opens, with the title List of uploaded files. It lists the files that the offices have uploaded for the request. The columns are:

  • #: the number of the file in the list.
  • Bus No: the number of the business.
  • Assessment Code: the number of the request.
  • Description: what the file is.
  • Office: the office that uploaded the file.
  • Action: the actions for the file.

When no office has uploaded a file yet, the list says No record found. The Upload button at the top right is used to add a file to the list.

Figure 4.19. The Files page, with no uploaded files yet.

4.8 Review the assessment

Click the arrow icon of the request (highlighted).

Figure 4.20. Click the arrow icon (highlighted) to open the assessment of the request. The business names of other applicants are hidden.

The Assessment Request page opens. The path at the top reads Dashboard, Assessment and MP, Assessment Request. The first part shows the details of the request:

Figure 4.21. The details of the request at the top of the Assessment Request page.
  • Business Name and Control No.: the name of the business and the control number of the request.
  • Business Category and Business Type: for example TRADING and GENERAL MERCHANDISE.
  • Transaction Date, Transaction Type and Business Year: when and what kind of request.
  • Owner Type: for example Sole Proprietor.
  • Capitalization: the capital of the business, in pesos.
  • No. Of Employee: the number of employees.
  • Gross Sales For Year, Daily Sales Php and Daily Sales X 360 Days: the sales figures. They show 0.00 for a new business.
  • CTC Number, Date Of Issue and Address Of Issue: the community tax certificate details you entered when approving the request.

Below the details, the Assessment List shows the fees the system computed. Each row has an Account Code, a Description and an Amount. In the example the fees are Bldg. Inspection Fee, Business Plate, Business Tax - Gross Sales Tax, DOC Stamp, Garbage Fee, Health and Sanitary, Locational Clearance, Mayor's Permit, Municipal License Fee, Occupational Fee, Penalty, RPTS/Tax Clearance and Sign Board.

Figure 4.22. The Assessment List with the fees and their amounts.

At the end of the list, the totals are:

  • Total Assessment: the total of all the fees in the list.
  • Current Dues: the dues for the current period.
  • Amount payable to the BFP: the amount that is payable to the Bureau of Fire Protection.
  • Annually Dues: the annual dues.
Figure 4.23. The totals of the assessment, followed by the buttons at the bottom of the page.

The buttons at the bottom of the page are:

  • Assessment: greyed out. The assessment was already run when you approved the request, so this button cannot be used again.
  • Add/Edit Fees: add a fee to the assessment or change the amount of a fee.
  • For Approval: sends the assessment to BPLO for approval, with your remarks.
  • Cancel: leaves the page without sending.
Figure 4.24. The buttons at the bottom of the page: Assessment (greyed out), Add/Edit Fees, For Approval and Cancel.

4.9 Add or edit fees

Click Add/Edit Fees (Figure 4.24) to open the Fees For Installment page. The path at the top reads Dashboard, Add Fees. The page lists the fees of the assessment with their Account Code, Description and Amount. Each row has an edit icon and a trash icon. The two buttons at the top right are Back, which returns to the assessment, and New Fee (highlighted).

Figure 4.25. The Fees For Installment page. Click New Fee (highlighted) to add a fee.
Figure 4.26. The Back and New Fee buttons. Click New Fee (highlighted) to add a fee.

At the end of the list, Total Amount shows the total of all the fees.

Figure 4.27. The end of the list, with the Total Amount.

To add a fee

  1. Click New Fee. The Add Fees To Installment window opens with the fields Select Fee and Enter Amount.
  2. Click Select Fee and click the fee you want to add from the list. The list contains the fees of the system, for example Barangay Clearance Fee, Bldg. Inspection Fee, Business Plate, DOC Stamp, Garbage Fee, Health and Sanitary, Locational Clearance, Mayor's Permit, Municipal License Fee, Occupational Fee, Penalty, RPTS/Tax Clearance and Sign Board.
  3. Type the amount in Enter Amount.
  4. Click Save (highlighted), or click Close to leave without saving.
Figure 4.28. The Add Fees To Installment window, empty.
Figure 4.29. The Select Fee list.
Figure 4.30. The window filled in. Click Save (highlighted).

The amount must be 1 or more. If you leave Select Fee or Enter Amount empty, a red message appears (Fee is a required field. or Amount is a required field.). A fee that is already in the list cannot be added again (Fee already added.). After saving, the message Fee succesfully added to installment. appears and the fee is in the list.

To change the amount of a fee

  1. Click the edit icon of the fee (highlighted in Figure 4.31 and Figure 4.35). The Edit Fees Amount window opens with the current amount in Enter Amount.
  2. Type the new amount.
  3. Click Update (highlighted), or click Close to leave without changing the amount.
Figure 4.31. Click the edit icon (highlighted) of the fee you want to change.
Figure 4.32. The Edit Fees Amount window. Click Update (highlighted) to keep the new amount.

The same rules apply as when you add a fee: the amount must be 1 or more. After you click Update, the message Fee succesfully updated. appears.

To remove a fee

Click the trash icon in the row of the fee (highlighted in Figure 4.33 and Figure 4.35).

Figure 4.33. Click the trash icon (highlighted) of the fee you want to remove.

The fee is removed at once, with no window asking you to confirm. A green message appears at the lower left: Fee succesfully removed. (highlighted in Figure 4.34). The fee leaves the list.

Figure 4.34. The message after a fee is removed.
Warning. Removing a fee takes it out of the assessment. To put it back, click New Fee and add it again.
Figure 4.35. The edit icon and the trash icon (highlighted) of a fee.
Good to know. The fees you add or change here become part of the assessment. When you are done, click Back and review the totals before you send the assessment for approval.

4.10 Send the assessment for approval

When the fees are correct, send the assessment to the BPLO for approval. Point to the For Approval button. A tip appears that reads Approved to enable bplo actions. Click For Approval (highlighted).

Figure 4.36. The buttons at the bottom of the Assessment Request page. Click For Approval (highlighted).

The Remarks window opens. Type your remarks for the BPLO, then click Save (highlighted), or click Cancel to close the window without sending.

  • Remarks is required. If you click Save with the box empty, a red message appears at the lower left: Remarks is a required field.
Figure 4.37. The Remarks window with the box left empty. Click Save (highlighted) and the message Remarks is a required field appears.
Figure 4.38. The Remarks window with the remarks typed. Click Save (highlighted).

After you click Save, a green message appears at the lower left: Request successfully added to assessment (highlighted). This is the wording of the system.

Figure 4.39. The message after the assessment is sent for approval.

The request is now with the BPLO. On the Assessment Request page, the buttons Assessment, Add/Edit Fees and For Approval turn gray and cannot be clicked. Only Cancel stays available, which leaves the page.

Figure 4.40. The buttons after the assessment is sent. Only Cancel can still be clicked.

Back in the For Assessment list, the Assessment Status of the request now reads FOR APPROVAL (highlighted).

Figure 4.41. The For Assessment list. The Assessment Status of the request is FOR APPROVAL (highlighted).
