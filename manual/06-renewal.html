Section 6

Requesting a Permit: Renewal

A Renewal request is for a business renewing its permit. The steps are similar to a new business request (Section 5), with the differences described here.

In this section
  • 6.1 Open the request form
  • 6.2 Fill in the details
  • 6.3 Choose the payment mode
  • 6.4 Attach the requirements
  • 6.5 Send the request

6.1 Open the request form

Open the assessment list and click Request, as described in Section 5.1. On the Request Form, click Transaction Type and choose Renewal.

6.2 Fill in the details

For a renewal, the form asks for the Gross Income of the business instead of its Capitalization.

Figure 6.4. The Request Form with Renewal selected. The form asks for Gross Income.
  • Business Name: the name of your business. It is filled in for you and cannot be changed.
  • Business Category and Business Type (required): filled in from the business record. Change them only if they are wrong.
  • Transaction Year: the year of the request.
  • Transaction Type (required): choose Renewal.
  • Gross Income: the gross income of the business, in pesos.
  • No of Employees: the number of employees.
  • Total Floor Area (required): the floor area of the business premises. It must be at least 1.
  • No. of Storey (required): the number of storeys. It must be at least 1.
  • Contact No: the contact number of the business.
  • Owner Name: the name of the business owner.
  • Authorized Representative (required): the person authorized to transact for the business.

6.3 Choose the payment mode

Click Payment Mode to open the list. For a Renewal, you can choose Annual, Semi-Annual or Quarterly.

Figure 6.5. The Payment Mode list for a Renewal. All three payment modes are available.

Because you chose Semi-Annual or Quarterly, an extra field appears below Payment Mode so you can say which period you are paying for.

  • Semi-Annual: an Annual field appears. Choose the period from the list, for example 1st Annual.
  • Quarterly: a Select Quarter Type field appears. Choose Single Quarter or Multiple Quarter.
Figure 6.1. Semi-Annual selected. The Annual field lets you choose the period, here 1st Annual.

With Single Quarter, choose one quarter from the list: 1st Quarter, 2nd Quarter, 3rd Quarter or 4th Quarter. Quarters that cannot be chosen yet are greyed out. In the example, only the 1st Quarter can be chosen.

Figure 6.2. Quarterly with Single Quarter selected. The quarter list shows 1st Quarter, with the other quarters greyed out.

With Multiple Quarter, tick the box of each quarter you want to request: 1st Quarter, 2nd Quarter, 3rd Quarter and 4th Quarter.

Figure 6.3. Quarterly with Multiple Quarter selected. Tick the quarters you want to request.

6.4 Attach the requirements

For a Renewal in the example, the requirements are DTI and Title or Tax Dec. Attach each file as described in Section 5.2.3.

Figure 6.6. The bottom of the Request Form for a Renewal, with the DTI and Title or Tax Dec requirements attached.

6.5 Send the request

Click Request to send the request, or Cancel to close the form without sending. If a field needs correction, see Section 5.3.
