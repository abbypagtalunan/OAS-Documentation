3.1 The Dashboard

After you sign in, eBPLS opens the Dashboard, which is the Business Holdings page. This section describes what you see on it and how to use its menus.

3.1.1 Overview

The Dashboard has three areas:

  • Top bar (blue, across the top): chat, notifications, settings and your account menu. See Section 3.1.2.
  • Sidebar (left side): links to the Dashboard, Payment History and other applications. See Section 3.1.3.
  • Main content: the Business Holdings page, where your businesses are listed. See Section 3.1.4.

3.2 Adding a New Business

Before you can request a permit, your business must be listed under your account. This section shows how to add a new business using Add New Company.

3.2.1 Before you begin

Have the following information ready:

  • The business name, franchise name, category and type, and a description of the business.
  • The complete business address, including the barangay.
  • The business email address.
  • The DTI registration number and the date it was issued.
  • The owner's name, address and gender.
  • The number of employees and the date the business started.
Required fields. You must complete the following fields before the business can be saved. Fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the form are required; Business Category, Owner Type, Barangay and Business Line Desc. are also required even though they have no asterisk.
  • General Information: Business Name, Franchise Name, Business Category, Business Type, Owner Type, Business Line Desc., Barangay, Complete Address, Company Email.
  • Registration: DTI No., DTI Date Issued.
  • Registered Owner: Owner, Owner Address, Owner Gender, No. of Employee (at least 1), Date Start.
  • Miscellaneous: none.
Text you type in most fields is converted to capital letters automatically.

3.2.2 Fill out the new business form

  1. On the Dashboard, click Add New Company (see Section 3.1.4).
Figure 3.7. Click Add New Company (highlighted) on the Business Holdings page.
  1. The New Business page opens. The path at the top of the page reads Dashboard, Management, Companies, Create.
  2. Fill in the four sections of the form, one after the other, as described in 4.2.1 to 4.2.4. Their names appear in the left column, highlighted in red in the figures.
  • General Information: the business name, category, type, address and contact details.
  • Registration: the DTI registration and other identification numbers.
  • Registered Owner: the owner and representative details, the business size and the key dates.
  • Miscellaneous: rental and other business-specific details.

3.2.2.1 General Information

This is the first section of the form.

Figure 3.8. The New Business page, showing the General Information section.
  • Business Name (required): the registered name of the business.
  • Franchise Name (required): the franchise name of the business.
  • Business Category (required): choose the category from the list. A category may already be selected when the form opens; change it to match your business.
  • Business Type (required): choose the type from the list. The list depends on the category you chose.
  • Owner Type (required): choose Corporation, Cooperative, Foundation, Partnership or Sole Proprietor.
  • Business Line Desc. (required): describe what the business does.
  • Street, Lot/Block No. and Street Code: the street, lot or block number and street code of the business address. Street Code starts at 0.
  • Barangay (required): choose the barangay from the list.
  • Province and Municipality: pre-filled with ALBAY and OAS. Change them only if the business is elsewhere.
  • Complete Address (required): the full address of the business.
  • Telephone No. and Fax No.: contact numbers of the business.
  • Company Email (required): the business email address. It must be a valid email.
  • Latitude and Longitude: the map coordinates of the business location.
Figure 3.9. The rest of the General Information section (Complete Address to Longitude), followed by the Registration section.

3.2.2.2 Registration

The Registration section follows General Information (see the lower part of Figure 3.9).

  • DTI No. (required): the DTI registration number.
  • DTI Date Issued (required): the date the DTI registration was issued. Choose it from the calendar.
  • TIN, PhilHealth, SSS and HDMF: the business identification numbers. SSS and HDMF accept up to 15 characters.
  • SEC Registration No. and SEC Date Issued: these two fields appear only when the Owner Type is not Sole Proprietor.

3.2.2.3 Registered Owner

Figure 3.10. The Registered Owner section.
Figure 3.11. The end of the Registered Owner section (signboard and dates), followed by the Miscellaneous section.
  • Owner (required): the full name of the business owner.
  • Owner Address (required): the owner's address.
  • Owner Gender (required): choose Male, Female or N/A.
  • Mobile No.: the owner's mobile number.
  • President and Treasurer: for organizations, the names of the president and the treasurer.
  • Representative, Representative Address and Representative Position: the person who represents the business, with address and position.
  • Capitalization: the capital of the business, in pesos.
  • No. of Employee (required): the number of employees. Enter at least 1.
  • Total Floor Area and No. of Storey: the floor area and the number of storeys of the business premises.
  • Using Signboard: choose Yes or No. If you choose Yes, an Area of Signboard field appears.
  • Date Start (required): the date the business started operating. Choose it from the calendar.
  • Date Applied, Date Renew, Date of Transfer and Date Closed: choose each date from the calendar. Leave them empty unless they apply.

3.2.2.4 Miscellaneous

Figure 3.12. The Miscellaneous section.
  • Rented: choose Yes if the business premises are rented. If you choose Yes, Since When, Monthly Rental and From Whom appear.
  • Office Type, Type Of Bank and Type Of Club: type-specific details. Leave them at 0 if they do not apply to your business.
  • No. of Heads If Farm: for farms, the number of heads. Leave at 0 if it does not apply.
  • Bedroom Type, No. of Bedrooms, No. Of Occupancy and With AC: for lodging businesses, the bedroom details. Leave them at 0 if they do not apply.
Figure 3.13. The end of the form, with the Cancel and Add Company buttons.

3.2.3 Saving the business

At the bottom of the form there are two buttons:

Figure 3.14. The Cancel and Add Company buttons.
  • Add Company: saves the business. A green message, Company Created, appears and you are taken back to the Business Holdings page, where the new business now appears in the list.
  • Cancel: closes the form without saving and returns to the Business Holdings page.

If a required field is missing

If a required field is empty when you click Add Company, the business is not saved. The empty field is outlined in red and a red message appears under it, as shown below.

Figure 3.15. Required fields left empty, outlined in red with a message under each.
Error: Business name is required and Franchise name is required (as in the figure). Fill in every field that shows a red message and click Add Company again. Scroll through all four sections, because a field with a message may be above or below the part of the form you are viewing.

The messages you may see are:

  • Business name is required: appears under Business Name.
  • Franchise name is required: appears under Franchise Name.
  • Business category is required and Business type is required: appear under Business Category and Business Type.
  • Owner type is required: appears under Owner Type.
  • Business line description is required: appears under Business Line Desc.
  • Barangay is required: appears under Barangay.
  • Business address is required: appears under Complete Address.
  • Company email is required and Invalid email address: appear under Company Email.
  • Owner is required, Owner address is required and Owner gender is required: appear under Owner, Owner Address and Owner Gender.
  • No. of employee is required: appears under No. of Employee.
  • Date start is required: appears under Date Start.

3.2.4 After the business is added

After you click Add Company, the system returns you to the Business Holdings page. The business you just added now appears in the list, and the page count at the bottom changes from 0-0 of 0 to 1-1 of 1.

Figure 3.16. The Business Holdings page showing the newly added business (highlighted in red).

The first column of the row, Assessment Request, has three icons:

  • Edit Business (pencil icon): opens the business record so you can review or change its details.
  • Step One - Display Assessment List (numbered 1 icon): opens the business's assessment list, where permit requests are made. A label with this name appears next to the icon while the business has no assessment request yet.
  • Location (pin icon): shows the business location.
If the numbered icon is greyed out, the business record is missing required details. Hover over the icon to read the message, then click Edit Business to complete the details.

3.2.5 Viewing the business details

The business you added appears on the Business Holdings page. The table has four tabs: General Info, Registration, Registered Owner and Miscellaneous. Click a tab to see a different set of details for each business. The Assessment Request and Business columns stay on every tab. If the table is wider than your screen, scroll sideways to see all columns.

3.2.5.1 General Info tab

Figure 3.17. The General Info tab.
  • Assessment Request: the icons for the business (see Section 3.2.4).
  • Business: the business name.
  • Franchise Name: the franchise name of the business.
  • Line Of Business: what the business does.
  • Complete Address: the full address of the business.
  • Business Type Code: the business type, such as General Merchandise.
  • Business Code: the number the system assigned to the business.
  • Owner Type: the owner type, shown as a letter, such as S for Sole Proprietor.
  • Street, Barangay, Street Code and Lot/Block No: the parts of the business address.

3.2.5.2 Registration tab

Figure 3.18. The Registration tab.
  • SEC Registration No and SEC Date Issued: the SEC details. They are empty for a sole proprietor.
  • DTI Number and DTI Date Issued: the DTI registration number and the date it was issued.
  • TIN, PhilHealth, SSS and HDMF: the business identification numbers, when provided.

3.2.5.3 Registered Owner tab

Figure 3.19. The Registered Owner tab.
  • Owner, Address, Gender and Mobile: the details of the business owner. Gender is shown as a letter, such as F for Female.
  • President/GM, Treasurer, Representative and Representative Position: the officers and representative of the business, when provided.

3.2.5.4 Miscellaneous tab

Figure 3.20. The Miscellaneous tab.
  • Rented: Y if the business premises are rented.
  • Since when, Monthly Rental and From Whom: the rental details.
  • Type of Office, Type of Bank and Type of Club: type-specific details. They show 0 when they do not apply.
  • Bedroom Type, No. of Occupancy, No. of Bedrooms and With A/C: the details for lodging businesses. They show 0 when they do not apply.

The next section explains how to open the assessment list and request a permit.

3.3 Requesting a Permit: New Business

A New Business request is for a business applying for a permit for the first time. This section shows how to open the assessment list for your business, fill out the request form, attach the requirements and send the request. Requests for a renewal, a closure or a transfer are explained in their own sections: Section 3.4, Section 3.5 and Section 3.6.

3.3.1 Open the assessment list

  1. On the Business Holdings page, find your business in the list.
  2. Click the numbered 1 icon in the Assessment Request column. A label, Step One - Display Assessment List, appears next to it while the business has no request yet.
Figure 3.21. Click the numbered 1 icon (Step One - Display Assessment List) in the business row, highlighted in red.

The numbered 1 icon appears only while the business has no request yet. After a request exists, it is replaced by an arrow. Click the arrow to open the Assessment Information page.

Figure 3.22. When the business already has a request, the numbered 1 icon is replaced by an arrow. Click the arrow to open the Assessment Information page.

The Assessment Information page opens. A new business has no request yet, so the page says No assessment found. and shows the Request button at the top right, with the label Step Two - Create Assessment Request next to it.

Figure 3.23. The Assessment Information page of a business with no request yet.

3.3.2 Fill out the request form

Click Request. The Request For Assessment page opens with the Request Form. The path at the top of the page reads Dashboard, Business, Assessment, Request. The page also says You have no request recorded for this year. when no request exists yet for the year.

The form has three parts, which you complete in this order: the details (Section 3.3.2.1), the payment mode (Section 3.3.2.2) and the requirements to attach (Section 3.3.2.3).

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required. Some details are filled in from the business record. Check them before you continue.

3.3.2.1 Fill in the details

Click Transaction Type to open the list and choose New Business.

Figure 3.24. The Transaction Type list. Choose New Business.

Then complete the form from top to bottom:

Figure 3.25. The top of the Request Form for a New Business.
  • Business Name: the name of your business. It is filled in for you and cannot be changed.
  • Business Category and Business Type (required): filled in from the business record. Change them only if they are wrong.
  • Transaction Year: the year of the request.
  • Transaction Type (required): choose New Business.
  • Capitalization: the capital of the business, in pesos.
  • No of Employees: the number of employees.
  • Total Floor Area (required): the floor area of the business premises. It must be at least 1.
  • No. of Storey (required): the number of storeys. It must be at least 1.
  • Contact No: the contact number of the business.
  • Owner Name: the name of the business owner.
  • Authorized Representative (required): the person authorized to transact for the business.
Figure 3.26. The Request Form filled in with sample details. The Attach Requirements section follows Payment Mode.

3.3.2.2 Choose the payment mode

Click Payment Mode to open the list. For a New Business, only Annual can be chosen. Semi-Annual and Quarterly are greyed out.

Figure 3.27. The Payment Mode list for a New Business. Only Annual is available.

3.3.2.3 Attach the requirements

Below Payment Mode, the Attach Requirements section lists the documents needed. This is marked Step 3 on the page. For a New Business in the example, they are Sketch and Photos of location, DTI and Title or Tax Dec. A requirement with an asterisk (*) is required.

Figure 3.28. The Attach Requirements section, with a Select Files button for each requirement, and the Cancel and Request buttons.
  1. Click Select Files beside a requirement. The Select Attachment Method window opens.
  2. Choose how to attach the file:
    • Upload Files: click Choose Files and select one or more files from your device.
    • Camera: use your device camera to capture the document.
  3. Repeat for every requirement.
Figure 3.29. The Select Attachment Method window, with the Upload Files and Camera tabs.

Each file you attach is listed under its requirement with its file name and size. To remove a file, click the trash icon at the right of the file.

Figure 3.30. The requirements with their files attached. Each file has a trash icon for removing it.

3.3.3 Send the request

At the bottom of the form:

Figure 3.31. Click Request (highlighted) at the bottom of the form to send the request.
  • Request: sends the request.
  • Cancel: closes the form without sending.

If something is missing

If a required field is empty or incorrect, the request is not sent. The field is outlined in red and a red message appears under it. Every requirement marked with an asterisk must also have a file attached.

Figure 3.32. The Request Form with messages under the fields that need correction.
Error: correct each field that shows a red message, then click Request again. The messages shown in the figure are:
  • area must be greater than or equal to 1: appears under Total Floor Area when it is 0.
  • no_of_storey must be greater than or equal to 1: appears under No. of Storey when it is 0.
  • Authorized representative is required: appears under Authorized Representative.
  • Payment mode is required: appears under Payment Mode.

3.3.4 After the request is sent

After you click Request, a green message, Assessment Sent, appears at the lower left of the screen while the next page loads. This means your request has been sent.

Figure 3.33. The Assessment Sent message at the lower left, while the next page loads.

The Assessment Requirements page then opens, with a reminder: Kindly complete the assessment requirements to avoid any delays in the process. Click Close to dismiss the reminder.

Figure 3.34. The reminder shown when the Assessment Requirements page opens.

3.3.5 Complete the assessment requirements

The Assessment Requirements page lists the offices that review your request. In the example these are Assessor, Engineering, Fire, Peso, Mao, Menro, Mpdo, Rhu and Bplo, followed by the barangay of the business (here, Barangay Badbad). This is Step 3 of the process: sending the attachments each office needs.

You can open this page from the Assessment Information list. After you send a request, it appears in the list with the status PENDING. Click the numbered 3 icon (Step Three - Sent Attachment Per Office) in the Action column of your request.

Figure 3.35. The Assessment Information list after the request is sent. The new request has the status PENDING, and the numbered 3 icon opens the Assessment Requirements page.
Figure 3.36. The Assessment Requirements page, with one row for each office.

At the top right of the page:

  • Back: returns to the previous page.
  • Attachment On Request: shows the files you attached when you sent the request (see Section 3.3.2.3). If you attached none, it says No attachment found.
Check the requirements of every office and upload the file needed for each requirement. Requirements that are not sent can delay your request.

Open an office and read its requirements

Click the name of an office, or the arrow at the right of its row, to open its Requirements List.

Figure 3.37. An office opened on the Assessment Requirements page, showing its Requirements List with the requirements still UNSENT.

The list has these columns:

  • Id: the number of the requirement.
  • Requirement: the document the office needs.
  • Required: TRUE means you must send this document.
  • Status: UNSENT (red) means you have not sent the file yet. SENT (green) means it has been sent.
  • Forms: No form included. means there is nothing to download. If the office provides a form, its file name appears here. Click it to download the form, fill it in and attach it.
  • Action: click Attach File to send the file. After a file is sent, a picture icon (View uploaded requirements) also appears.

An office that has no requirements for your business shows No requirements found. You do not need to send anything to that office.

Send the file for a requirement

  1. Click Attach File beside the requirement. The Upload Requirements Attachment window opens. It has two tabs, Upload Files and Camera.
Figure 3.38. The Upload Requirements Attachment window.
  1. On the Upload Files tab, drop the file into the Select files box, or click browse to choose it from your computer. The files must total less than 50 MB. You can add more than one file for the same requirement.
  2. Each chosen file is listed with its name and size. To take a file out, click the trash icon beside it. Click Remove all to clear every chosen file.
Figure 3.39. A chosen file listed in the window, with the trash icon and Remove all.
  1. Click Upload Requirements to send the file, or Cancel to close the window without sending.

When the file is sent, a green message, Email Sent, appears at the lower left. The Status of that requirement changes from UNSENT to SENT, and a picture icon (View uploaded requirements) appears in the Action column. Click it to see what you sent.

Figure 3.40. The DTI requirement is now SENT, with the picture icon. The other requirements are still UNSENT.

Repeat for every requirement of every office. When you finish an office, every requirement in its list shows SENT.

Figure 3.41. All requirements of the office are SENT.

If a file cannot be sent, the message Email not sent. appears. Check your internet connection and try again.

Error: images is a required field appears in red in the window if you click Upload Requirements without choosing a file. Choose a file first, then click Upload Requirements again.
Figure 3.42. Another office, Fire, with both requirements SENT.

View the files you sent

After a requirement is sent, a picture icon (View uploaded requirements) appears in its Action column. Point to it to see its label, then click it.

Figure 3.43. The picture icon (highlighted) in the Action column. Point to it to see "View uploaded requirements".

The Uploaded Requirements Attachment window opens. Each file you sent for that requirement appears as a tile with its file type and file name. If you sent more than one file, all of them are shown. Click the X at the top right to close the window.

Figure 3.44. The Uploaded Requirements Attachment window, showing two files sent for one requirement.
Camera. The Camera tab lets you take a photo of the document directly with your device camera, for example on a phone or tablet. Your browser may ask for permission to use the camera.

3.4 Requesting a Permit: Renewal

A Renewal request is for a business renewing its permit. The steps are similar to a new business request (Section 3.3), with the differences described here.

3.4.2 Fill in the details

For a renewal, the form asks for the Gross Income of the business instead of its Capitalization.

Figure 3.59. The Request Form with Renewal selected. The form asks for Gross Income.
  • Business Name: the name of your business. It is filled in for you and cannot be changed.
  • Business Category and Business Type (required): filled in from the business record. Change them only if they are wrong.

3.8 Waiting for Approval: The Pending Status

After you send your request and its requirements, your request is reviewed in several steps. The status of the request stays PENDING in your Assessment Information list the whole time you wait. This part explains who reviews the request and what you can do meanwhile.

3.8.1 Who reviews your request

Three groups of staff work on your request. Until they have finished, the status does not change.

  1. The Permit Evaluator checks the request and its requirements, runs the assessment and sends it on for approval (Section 4).
  2. The offices (for example Engineering, Fire and Health) review the request and approve or disapprove it, each with remarks (Section 5).
  3. The Municipal Treasurer's Office reviews the fees of the assessment and gives the final approval (Section 6). In the status list of your request, this office appears as BPLO.

3.8.2 What you see while you wait

The Status column of the Assessment Information list shows PENDING in orange for your request. The print icon and the arrow icon in the Action column are gray, which means they are not available yet.

Figure 3.79. The Assessment Information list while the requests are waiting. The status of both requests is PENDING.

To see how far your request has come, click the PENDING status. The list shows each office with a colored dot: red when the office has not acted yet, green when it approved, and orange when it did not approve (Section 3.3.8). Click the arrow beside an office to read its remarks.

When every review is done and the request is approved, the status changes to FOR PAYMENT and the print icon and the arrow icon become available (Section 3.9). Payment is made on site.

3.8.3 What you can do while you wait

  • Check your notifications. The bell icon in the top bar shows updates about your request.
  • Send what is missing. If an office asks for another file, send it on the Assessment Requirements page (Section 3.3.5).
  • Ask a question. Click Send Inquiry to write to an office (Section 3.7).
A request that is still PENDING cannot be paid or released. You do not need to send the request again while you wait.
