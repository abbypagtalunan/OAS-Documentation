Section 5

Requesting a Permit: New Business

A New Business request is for a business applying for a permit for the first time. This section shows how to open the assessment list for your business, fill out the request form, attach the requirements and send the request. Requests for a renewal, a closure or a transfer are explained in their own sections: Section 6, Section 7 and Section 8.

5.1 Open the assessment list

  1. On the Business Holdings page, find your business in the list.
  2. Click the numbered 1 icon in the Assessment Request column. A label, Step One - Display Assessment List, appears next to it while the business has no request yet.
Figure 5.1. Click the numbered 1 icon (Step One - Display Assessment List) in the business row, highlighted in red.

The numbered 1 icon appears only while the business has no request yet. After a request exists, it is replaced by an arrow. Click the arrow to open the Assessment Information page.

Figure 5.2. When the business already has a request, the numbered 1 icon is replaced by an arrow. Click the arrow to open the Assessment Information page.

The Assessment Information page opens. A new business has no request yet, so the page says No assessment found. and shows the Request button at the top right, with the label Step Two - Create Assessment Request next to it.

Figure 5.3. The Assessment Information page of a business with no request yet.

5.2 Fill out the request form

Click Request. The Request For Assessment page opens with the Request Form. The path at the top of the page reads Dashboard, Business, Assessment, Request. The page also says You have no request recorded for this year. when no request exists yet for the year.

The form has three parts, which you complete in this order: the details (Section 5.2.1), the payment mode (Section 5.2.2) and the requirements to attach (Section 5.2.3).

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required. Some details are filled in from the business record. Check them before you continue.

5.2.1 Fill in the details

Click Transaction Type to open the list and choose New Business.

Figure 5.4. The Transaction Type list. Choose New Business.

Then complete the form from top to bottom:

Figure 5.5. The top of the Request Form for a New Business.
  • Business Name: the name of your business. It is filled in for you and cannot be changed.
  • Business Category and Business Type (required): filled in from the business record. Change them only if they are wrong.
  • Transaction Year: the year of the request.
  • Transaction Type (required): choose New Business.
  • Capitalization: the capital of the business, in pesos.
  • No of Employees: the number of employees.
  • Total Floor Area (required): the floor area of the business premises. It must be at least 1.
  • No. of Storey (required): the number of storeys. It must be at least 1.
  • Contact No: the contact number of the business.
  • Owner Name: the name of the business owner.
  • Authorized Representative (required): the person authorized to transact for the business.
Figure 5.6. The Request Form filled in with sample details. The Attach Requirements section follows Payment Mode.

5.2.2 Choose the payment mode

Click Payment Mode to open the list. For a New Business, only Annual can be chosen. Semi-Annual and Quarterly are greyed out.

Figure 5.7. The Payment Mode list for a New Business. Only Annual is available.

5.2.3 Attach the requirements

Below Payment Mode, the Attach Requirements section lists the documents needed. This is marked Step 3 on the page. For a New Business in the example, they are Sketch and Photos of location, DTI and Title or Tax Dec. A requirement with an asterisk (*) is required.

Figure 5.8. The Attach Requirements section, with a Select Files button for each requirement, and the Cancel and Request buttons.
  1. Click Select Files beside a requirement. The Select Attachment Method window opens.
  2. Choose how to attach the file:
    • Upload Files: click Choose Files and select one or more files from your device.
    • Camera: use your device camera to capture the document.
  3. Repeat for every requirement.
Figure 5.9. The Select Attachment Method window, with the Upload Files and Camera tabs.

Each file you attach is listed under its requirement with its file name and size. To remove a file, click the trash icon at the right of the file.

Figure 5.10. The requirements with their files attached. Each file has a trash icon for removing it.

5.3 Send the request

At the bottom of the form:

Figure 5.11. Click Request (highlighted) at the bottom of the form to send the request.
  • Request: sends the request.
  • Cancel: closes the form without sending.

If something is missing

If a required field is empty or incorrect, the request is not sent. The field is outlined in red and a red message appears under it. Every requirement marked with an asterisk must also have a file attached.

Figure 5.12. The Request Form with messages under the fields that need correction.
Error: correct each field that shows a red message, then click Request again. The messages shown in the figure are:
  • area must be greater than or equal to 1: appears under Total Floor Area when it is 0.
  • no_of_storey must be greater than or equal to 1: appears under No. of Storey when it is 0.
  • Authorized representative is required: appears under Authorized Representative.
  • Payment mode is required: appears under Payment Mode.

5.4 After the request is sent

After you click Request, a green message, Assessment Sent, appears at the lower left of the screen while the next page loads. This means your request has been sent.

Figure 5.13. The Assessment Sent message at the lower left, while the next page loads.

The Assessment Requirements page then opens, with a reminder: Kindly complete the assessment requirements to avoid any delays in the process. Click Close to dismiss the reminder.

Figure 5.14. The reminder shown when the Assessment Requirements page opens.

5.5 Complete the assessment requirements

The Assessment Requirements page lists the offices that review your request. In the example these are Assessor, Engineering, Fire, Peso, Mao, Menro, Mpdo, Rhu and Bplo, followed by the barangay of the business (here, Barangay Badbad). This is Step 3 of the process: sending the attachments each office needs.

You can open this page from the Assessment Information list. After you send a request, it appears in the list with the status PENDING. Click the numbered 3 icon (Step Three - Sent Attachment Per Office) in the Action column of your request.

Figure 5.15. The Assessment Information list after the request is sent. The new request has the status PENDING, and the numbered 3 icon opens the Assessment Requirements page.
Figure 5.16. The Assessment Requirements page, with one row for each office.

At the top right of the page:

  • Back: returns to the previous page.
  • Attachment On Request: shows the files you attached when you sent the request (see Section 5.2.3). If you attached none, it says No attachment found.
Check the requirements of every office and upload the file needed for each requirement. Requirements that are not sent can delay your request.

Open an office and read its requirements

Click the name of an office, or the arrow at the right of its row, to open its Requirements List.

Figure 5.17. An office opened on the Assessment Requirements page, showing its Requirements List with the requirements still UNSENT.

The list has these columns:

  • Id: the number of the requirement.
  • Requirement: the document the office needs.
  • Required: TRUE means you must send this document.
  • Status: UNSENT (red) means you have not sent the file yet. SENT (green) means it has been sent.
  • Forms: No form included. means there is nothing to download. If the office provides a form, its file name appears here. Click it to download the form, fill it in and attach it.
  • Action: click Attach File to send the file. After a file is sent, a picture icon (View uploaded requirements) also appears.

An office that has no requirements for your business shows No requirements found. You do not need to send anything to that office.

Send the file for a requirement

  1. Click Attach File beside the requirement. The Upload Requirements Attachment window opens. It has two tabs, Upload Files and Camera.
Figure 5.18. The Upload Requirements Attachment window.
  1. On the Upload Files tab, drop the file into the Select files box, or click browse to choose it from your computer. The files must total less than 50 MB. You can add more than one file for the same requirement.
  2. Each chosen file is listed with its name and size. To take a file out, click the trash icon beside it. Click Remove all to clear every chosen file.
Figure 5.19. A chosen file listed in the window, with the trash icon and Remove all.
  1. Click Upload Requirements to send the file, or Cancel to close the window without sending.

When the file is sent, a green message, Email Sent, appears at the lower left. The Status of that requirement changes from UNSENT to SENT, and a picture icon (View uploaded requirements) appears in the Action column. Click it to see what you sent.

Figure 5.20. The DTI requirement is now SENT, with the picture icon. The other requirements are still UNSENT.

Repeat for every requirement of every office. When you finish an office, every requirement in its list shows SENT.

Figure 5.21. All requirements of the office are SENT.

If a file cannot be sent, the message Email not sent. appears. Check your internet connection and try again.

Error: images is a required field appears in red in the window if you click Upload Requirements without choosing a file. Choose a file first, then click Upload Requirements again.
Figure 5.22. Another office, Fire, with both requirements SENT.

View the files you sent

After a requirement is sent, a picture icon (View uploaded requirements) appears in its Action column. Point to it to see its label, then click it.

Figure 5.23. The picture icon (highlighted) in the Action column. Point to it to see "View uploaded requirements".

The Uploaded Requirements Attachment window opens. Each file you sent for that requirement appears as a tile with its file type and file name. If you sent more than one file, all of them are shown. Click the X at the top right to close the window.

Figure 5.24. The Uploaded Requirements Attachment window, showing two files sent for one requirement.
Camera. The Camera tab lets you take a photo of the document directly with your device camera, for example on a phone or tablet. Your browser may ask for permission to use the camera.

5.6 Return to the Assessment Information list

When you have checked the requirements of every office and sent the files you need, go back to the Assessment Information list. Click Back at the top right of the Assessment Requirements page, or click Business in the path at the top of the page (Dashboard, Business, Assessment).

Figure 5.35. Click Back (highlighted) at the top right to return to the Assessment Information list.

5.7 View the Assessment Information list

The Assessment Information page lists your requests for the business. Each request is one row.

Figure 5.36. The Assessment Information list. The request has the status PENDING.

The columns are:

  • Action: the icons for the request (see below).
  • Status: the status of the request, for example PENDING. Click it to see the status of each office (see Section 5.8).
  • Assessment Code: the number of the request.
  • Date Requested and Transaction Year: when and for which year you made the request.
  • Transaction Type: the kind of request, for example New Business.
  • Capitalization and Gross Income: the amount you entered. A new business shows its capitalization, and a renewal shows its gross income.
  • Payment Mode and Payment Quarter: how you will pay and, for a quarterly request, the quarter.

The icons in the Action column are:

Figure 5.37. The icons in the Action column: Send Inquiry, Offices Uploaded Files, View/Sent Attachments, Assessment Details and View/Print Assessment.
  • Send Inquiry (paper plane): opens the inquiry page so you can send a question to the offices (see Section 9).
  • Offices Uploaded Files (upload icon): shows the files the offices have uploaded for your request.
  • View/Sent Attachments (paperclip): opens the Assessment Requirements page again, where you can check and send requirements. Until you send your first file, this icon is the numbered 3 icon (Step Three - Sent Attachment Per Office).
  • Assessment Details (print icon): opens the assessment so you can print it. It is greyed out until the request has been approved.
  • View/Print Assessment (arrow icon): opens the assessment details. It is greyed out until the request has been approved.

5.8 Check the status of your request

Click the status of the request (for example PENDING) to see where it stands. A list opens with one row for each office: Fire, Engineering, Assessor, Rhu, Mpdo, Menro, Mao, Peso, Barangay and Bplo.

Figure 5.38. The status of each office. A red dot means the office has not acted on the request yet.
  • A red dot means the office has not acted on your request yet. All offices show red when a request is new.
  • A green dot means the office has approved your request.
  • An orange dot means the office has not approved your request.
  • Click the arrow at the right of an office to read its remarks. If the office left none, it says No remarks.

5.9 Wait for the approval of the offices

After you send your request and its requirements, the offices review it. All the offices must approve your request before it can move forward, so you need to wait for each one.

  • Check the progress. Click the status of your request in the Assessment Information list to see the status of each office (Section 5.8). The dot of an office changes from red when the office acts on your request. Click the arrow beside an office to read its remarks. The offices are described in Section 11.
  • Watch your notifications. The bell icon in the top bar shows updates about your request.
  • If an office needs something more. Read its remarks, then send the missing or corrected files on the Assessment Requirements page (Section 5.5). You can also click Send Inquiry in the Action column to ask the office a question (see Section 9).
Keep checking the status of your request until every office has approved it. A request that is waiting on one office cannot be paid or released.
