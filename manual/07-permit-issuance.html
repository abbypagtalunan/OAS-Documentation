Section 7

Permit Issuance: Preparing the Mayor's Permit

After the payment is processed, the Permit Issuance staff prepare the Mayor's Permit. This section describes the account, the lists on its dashboard, and the steps to assign the plate and license numbers, print the permit and upload it for the applicant.

7.1 The Permit Issuance account

The Permit Issuance staff sign in with the account named eBPLS Permit Issuance. They take over a request after the Municipal Treasurer's Office has processed the payment (Section 6.7). They assign the plate and license numbers, print the Mayor's Permit and upload it so that the applicant can download it (Section 3.10).

The sidebar

  • Mayors Permit: the dashboard, the Assessment Request page.
  • Reports: Companies, Requirements Lists, Registered Businesses, Index Of Payments, Closed Businesses, Bus Category Per-Account, Business Summary, and Summary of Collection per Barangay. They are the same reports as those of the Permit Evaluator and of the Municipal Treasurer's Office, which are described in 7.6.
  • Applications: Mail, Chat and Calendar.

The dashboard

The Assessment Request page has four lists. Click a list to open it. The number beside a list is how many requests it holds.

  • For MP Preparation: requests whose permit is to be prepared (7.2).
  • MP For Release: permits that are ready for release (7.5).
  • Semi-Annual/Quarter Transaction: requests that are paid in parts.
  • Closure Transaction: requests to close a business.
Figure 7.1. The dashboard of the Permit Issuance account, with the account name highlighted in red. The other applicants' businesses are hidden.

The Semi-Annual/Quarter Transaction and Closure Transaction lists have the same columns: Date, Business, Period, Quarter, Type, Is Released, Receiver and Action, with Search business and the Year box. They show No record found. when there are no requests of that kind.

Figure 7.2. The Semi-Annual/Quarter Transaction list, empty.
Figure 7.3. The Closure Transaction list, empty.

7.2 For MP Preparation

Click For MP Preparation to open the list. Use Search business and the Year box to find a request. The columns are Date, Business, Period, Quarter, Type, License No., Plate No., OR Number (the number of the official receipt of the payment), Paid Amount, MP Printed and Action. Scroll the table sideways to see all of them.

The Action column has five icons: a refresh icon, a document icon, a paperclip icon, a plus icon and a print icon. The plus icon assigns the plate and license numbers (7.3). The print icon is gray until the numbers are assigned, and it opens the permit (7.4).

Figure 7.4. The For MP Preparation list. The first request has its plate and license numbers, so its print icon is available. The other applicants' businesses are hidden.

7.3 Assign the plate and license number

A new request has no License No. and no Plate No., and its print icon is gray. Assign the numbers first.

  1. Point to the plus icon (highlighted). The tip reads Assign Plate Number and License Number. Click it.
  2. The Assign Plate and License Number window opens. Type the plate number in Enter plate number and the license number in Enter license number.
  3. Click Save (highlighted), or click Cancel to close the window.
Figure 7.5. The Action icons of a request without numbers. Click the plus icon (highlighted).
Figure 7.6. The tip of the plus icon: Assign Plate Number and License Number.
Figure 7.7. The window with the numbers typed. Click Save (highlighted).

A green message appears at the lower left: Plate number and license number successfully added. The numbers now show in the License No. and Plate No. columns, and the print icon is available.

Figure 7.8. The message after the numbers are saved (highlighted).

7.4 Print and upload the permit

  1. Click the print icon (highlighted) in the row of the request. The Mayor's Permit opens as a Business Permit page for the year.
  2. Check the permit. It shows the permit number, the owner and the business, the dates, the OR Number and OR Date, and a QR code.
  3. Click Print Permit (highlighted) at the top right to print the permit.
  4. Click Attach/Upload Permit (highlighted) to upload the permit to the files of the applicant.
Figure 7.9. The For MP Preparation list. Click the print icon (highlighted) of the request.
Figure 7.10. The buttons of the permit page. Click Attach/Upload Permit (highlighted) after printing.

After the upload, a green message appears: Mayors permit uploaded successfully. The Attach/Upload Permit button turns gray. The applicant then sees the status PERMIT IS READY FOR PICKUP and can open the permit from the Files page (Section 3.10.2).

Figure 7.11. The Business Permit page after the upload. The Print Permit and Attach/Upload Permit buttons and the message are highlighted. The name and signature of the Municipal Mayor are hidden.

7.5 MP For Release

Click MP For Release to open the list of permits that are ready for release. Use Search business and the Year box to find a request. The columns are Date, Business, Period, Quarter, Type, Is Released (Yes when the permit was released), Receiver (who received the permit) and Action. The Action column has a refresh icon, a plus icon (gray here), a document icon and a three-dot menu.

Figure 7.12. The MP For Release list. The business name of the other applicant is hidden.

7.6 Reports

The Reports group in the sidebar holds the lists and reports of the office. Click an item to open it. Each item is described below.

Companies

Click Companies to open the List of Business, the list of all the businesses in the system. Use Search business and click Search to find a business. The columns are a tick box, the edit icon, Bus Code, Business Name, Owner, Owner Type and Link Accounts (the lock icon). The top right has the buttons View Map and New Company.

Figure 7.13. The List of Business under Companies. The businesses and owners of other applicants are hidden.

Click the lock icon to see the user accounts linked to a business. When none is linked, the window reads Users account users this business. and No users linked to this business. Click Close to close it.

Figure 7.14. The window opened by the lock icon.

To see businesses on the map, click the tick box of one or more businesses. View Map is gray until a business is ticked; then it turns on. Click it to open the map, use the + and - buttons to zoom, and click View List (highlighted) to return to the list.

Figure 7.15. The map. Click View List (highlighted) to return to the list.

Requirements Lists

Click Requirements Lists to open the List Of Requirements of this office (permit-issuance in the Office column). The list has a search box and the columns Id, Office, Business Type, Requirements, Attachments and Action (edit and trash icons). In the example the requirements are C-01 BPLO, R-01 BPLO and N-01 BPLO.

Figure 7.16. The List Of Requirements of the Permit Issuance office.

To add a requirement, fill in the New Requirements form at the right, then click Save:

  • Requirement: the name of the requirement. If it is empty when you click Save, the field turns red and a message appears under it.
  • Is Required: choose true if the applicant must send it.
  • Is Default: choose Yes or No.
  • Select Business Type: choose the business type from the list. You can type to narrow the list.
  • Attachments: click Choose Files to attach a form, if there is one.
  • The tick boxes New, Renewal, Transfer and Closure: tick the kinds of request the requirement is for.
  • Cancel clears the form.
Figure 7.17. The form with Requirement empty. The field is red and a message appears under it.
Figure 7.18. The Select Business Type list.

Click the edit icon of a requirement to open the Edit Requirements window with the same fields and the tick boxes. Click Save to keep the changes, or Cancel. The trash icon removes a requirement. The full steps are in Section 5.10.

Figure 7.19. The Edit Requirements window of the requirement C-01 BPLO, with Closure ticked.

Registered Businesses

Click Registered Businesses to open the List of Registered Businesses. To make a report, type a name in Search business if you want one business only, choose the two dates, and click Generate (highlighted). The report has the columns Business Name, Owners Name, Owners Gender, Transaction Date, Business Address (Barangay), Business Line, Telephone Number, Nature, Mode of Payment, Business Category, Owner Type and No. of employee, and the totals Total # of Employees, Total Capitalization and Total Gross at the end. Scroll the table sideways to see all the columns.

Figure 7.20. The List of Registered Businesses after Generate (highlighted). The names and gender of other applicants are hidden.

With a shorter period, the report lists only the businesses of that period.

Figure 7.21. The report for October only, with one business. The gender is hidden.

Click Download Excel to download the report as an Excel file. Click Print Report to open the print window of the browser with a preview. Choose a printer, or click PDF at the lower left to save the report as a PDF file, then click Print, or click Cancel.

Figure 7.22. The print window with a preview of the report.

Index Of Payments

Click Index Of Payments to open the List of Businesses of the index of payments. Use Search business and the Year box to narrow the list, and click Download Excel to download it. The columns are Business Name, Owners Name, Transaction Date, Business Address (Barangay), Business Line, Telephone Number, Nature, Business Category and Owner Type. Each row ends with an arrow icon that opens the payments of that business. The rows per page and the page arrows are at the bottom.

Figure 7.23. The List of Businesses under Index Of Payments. The businesses and owners of other applicants are hidden.

Closed Businesses

Click Closed Businesses to open the List of Closed Businesses. Search by business name, choose the two dates and click Generate (highlighted). Download Excel and Print Report are gray when the report has no records. The columns are Date Closed, Business Name, Owners Name, Business Address (Barangay) and Business Line. The page shows No record found. when no business closed in the period.

Figure 7.24. The List of Closed Businesses with no record.

Bus Category Per-Account

Click Bus Category Per-Account to open the Business Category per Account report. Choose the two dates and click Generate, then Download Excel or Print Report. The report lists, for each business category (for example TRADING and SERVICES), one line for each fee account with the amount for the period, and ends each category with its total, such as TOTAL TRADING and TOTAL SERVICES.

Figure 7.25. The top of the report, with the category TRADING.
Figure 7.26. The end of TRADING and the category SERVICES, each with its total.

Business Summary

Click Business Summary to open the Summary Report. Choose the two dates and click Generate (highlighted). The report shows the Number of Business Establishments (new and renewed), the number by nature of business, and the Amount of Investment table with Gross, Capital, Collections and No. Of Employee for new and renewed businesses. It ends with Prepared by: Licensing Officer and Noted by: Municipal Mayor.

Figure 7.27. The Summary Report after Generate (highlighted).

Summary of Collection per Barangay

Click Summary of Collection per Barangay to open the report of the money collected in each barangay. The page has the title Summary Report. Choose the dates and click Generate (highlighted). The report Summary Of Collections has the columns Barangay, Total Collected and Total Number Of Tax Payer, one row for each barangay, and a Grand Total row.

Figure 7.28. The Summary of Collections report after Generate (highlighted).
