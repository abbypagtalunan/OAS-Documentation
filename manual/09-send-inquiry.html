Section 9

Sending an Inquiry

Use an inquiry to ask an office a question about your business or your request. The office answers in the same conversation.

In this section
  • 9.1 Open the Inquiries page
  • 9.2 Make an inquiry
  • 9.3 View your inquiries and continue the conversation

9.1 Open the Inquiries page

In the Assessment Information list, click the Send Inquiry icon (the paper plane) in the Action column.

Figure 9.1. Click the Send Inquiry icon (highlighted) in the Action column of the Assessment Information list.

The Inquiries page opens. If you have not sent any inquiry yet, the List Of Inquiries says No record found. The page has these parts:

  • Watch Guide Video: plays the guide video.
  • Make Inquiry: opens the window where you write a new inquiry.
  • List Of Inquiries: lists your inquiries. Use the Search box to find one.
Figure 9.2. The Inquiries page with no inquiries yet. Click Make Inquiry (highlighted) to write one.

9.2 Make an inquiry

  1. Click Make Inquiry (highlighted in Figure 9.2). The Make Inquiry window opens.
Figure 9.3. The Make Inquiry window, with Select Business, Select Office and Enter message inquiry.
  1. Click Select Business and choose your business from the list.
Figure 9.4. The Select Business list shows your businesses.
  1. Click Select Office and choose the office you want to ask. The list has Assessor, Engineering, Fire, Peso, Mao, Menro, Mpdo, Rhu and Bplo, and the barangay of your business.
Figure 9.5. The Select Office list. In the example, the barangay of the business is Barangay Badbad.
  1. Type your question in Enter message inquiry.
  2. Click Send (highlighted), or Cancel to close the window without sending.
Figure 9.6. The Make Inquiry window filled in. Click Send (highlighted) to send the inquiry.

A green message, Inquiry successfully sent., appears at the lower left, and the inquiry appears in the List Of Inquiries. It is listed by the name of the office, with the beginning of your message.

Figure 9.7. The Inquiries page after the inquiry is sent. The inquiry is listed under the office name.
Figure 9.8. Clicking Send (highlighted) with the form incomplete shows a red message at the lower left. In the figure, the message is "Inquery is a required field."
Error: if you click Send without choosing an office or without writing a message, the inquiry is not sent and a red message appears. The messages are Receiver is a required field. and Inquery is a required field. Choose the office, write your message and click Send again.

9.3 View your inquiries and continue the conversation

Click an inquiry in the List Of Inquiries to open it. The conversation opens on the right. It shows the business the inquiry is about and the date, and your message in a blue box. When the office replies, its reply appears in the same conversation.

Figure 9.9. An inquiry opened. Type in the Message box and click the send icon (highlighted) to send another message.

To send another message in the same conversation:

  1. Type your message in the Message box at the bottom.
  2. Click the send icon (the paper plane at the right of the box, highlighted).
Figure 9.10. Clicking the send icon (highlighted) with an empty Message box shows the red message "message is a required field."
Error: if you click the send icon with an empty box, a red message, message is a required field., appears and nothing is sent. Type your message and try again.
