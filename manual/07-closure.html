Section 7

Requesting a Permit: Closure

A Closure request is for a business that is closing. The steps are similar to a new business request (Section 5), with the differences described here.

7.1 Open the request form

Open the assessment list and click Request, as described in Section 5.1. On the Request Form, click Transaction Type and choose Closure.

7.2 Fill in the details

For a closure, the form does not ask for Capitalization or Gross Income. After Transaction Type, it goes straight to No of Employees.

Figure 7.1. The Request Form with Closure selected. There is no Capitalization or Gross Income field.
  • Business Name: the name of your business. It is filled in for you and cannot be changed.
  • Business Category and Business Type (required): filled in from the business record. Change them only if they are wrong.
  • Transaction Year: the year of the request.
  • Transaction Type (required): choose Closure.
  • No of Employees: the number of employees.
  • Total Floor Area (required): the floor area of the business premises. It must be at least 1.
  • No. of Storey (required): the number of storeys. It must be at least 1.
  • Contact No: the contact number of the business.
  • Owner Name: the name of the business owner.
  • Authorized Representative (required): the person authorized to transact for the business.

7.3 Choose the payment mode

For a Closure, only Annual can be chosen.

7.4 Attach the requirements

For a Closure in the example, the only requirement is DTI. Attach the file as described in Section 5.2.3.

Figure 7.2. The bottom of the Request Form for a Closure, with the DTI requirement attached.

7.5 Send the request

Click Request to send the request, or Cancel to close the form without sending. If a field needs correction, see Section 5.3.
