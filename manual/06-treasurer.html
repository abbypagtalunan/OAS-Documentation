6.1 About the Municipal Treasurer's Office

The Municipal Treasurer's Office signs in to eBPLS with the account named eBPLS Assessment. In the system this account is also known as Bplo (its page addresses begin with /bplo, and its office is listed as bplo in the Requirements list). This office reviews the assessment fees that the Permit Evaluator adds to a request, and it is the final approver of the request (Section 4.10 describes how the Permit Evaluator sends the assessment to this office).

This section first describes the screens of the account, then explains each item of the sidebar on its own. The steps for reviewing a request are in 6.6.

6.2 The interface

After you sign in, the Assessment Administrator Dashboard opens. The page has three parts: the top bar, the sidebar and the main content.

Figure 6.1. The dashboard of the Municipal Treasurer's Office, with the account name highlighted in red.

The top bar

  • The municipal seal at the left.
  • The chat icon and the bell icon at the right, for messages and notifications.
  • The gear icon, for settings.
  • The account name, eBPLS Assessment, with its initial. Click it to open the account menu.

The sidebar

The sidebar at the left shows the account name and the items you can click. The items are grouped as follows. Each item has its own part of this section.

  • Dashboard (6.3).
  • Reports (6.4): Companies, Requirements Lists, Request For Clearances, Holdings, Registered Businesses, Index Of Payments, Closed Businesses, Bus Category Per-Account, Business Summary, and Summary of Collection per Barangay.
  • Reference (6.5): Fees Account and Fees Template.
  • Applications: Mail, Chat and Calendar. The two with a purple Updated badge, Mail and Chat, have new activity.

The main content

The main content shows the page you chose. At the top of every page is the path (for example Dashboard, Requests) and the title of the page. The Watch Guide Video button on most pages plays a short video about the page.

6.3 Dashboard

Click Dashboard in the sidebar to return to the Assessment Administrator Dashboard (Figure 6.1). The main content has two parts.

  • The left side has four lists of requests. The number beside each list is how many requests it holds. Click a list to open it.
    • Pending: requests waiting for the decision of this office.
    • Disapproved: requests this office disapproved.
    • Approved: requests this office approved.
    • For MP Preparation: approved requests that go on to the preparation of the Mayor's Permit.
  • The right side is Request With Attachment. It lists the requests that came with an attachment, with the columns Business, Description, Status and Remarks. It shows No record found. when there are none.

Opening a request and reviewing its fees are described in 6.6.

  • The edit icon, to change the details of the business.
  • Bus Code, Business Name, Owner and Owner Type.
  • Link Accounts: the lock icon shows the user accounts linked to the business.

6.5 Reference

The Reference group holds the fees that the system uses to make an assessment. These are the fees the Permit Evaluator picks from when adding a fee (Section 4.9).

Fees Account

Click Fees Account to open the page of the fee accounts. A fee account is a fee, with its account code, that can be added to an assessment. The page has these parts:

  • Select Business Category at the top. Click it and choose the category: MANUFACTURING-NON ESSENTIAL, SERVICES or TRADING. The amounts shown further down belong to the category you choose.
  • The New Fees Account button at the top right, to add a fee account.
  • Fees Account Descriptions at the left: the list of fees, with a Search box. Each fee has an edit icon and an arrow icon. The list shows 10 fees per page, with the page arrows at the bottom.
  • The panel at the right shows the amounts of the fee you chose.
Figure 6.26. The Select Business Category list (highlighted).

To see the amounts of a fee

Click the arrow icon of the fee (highlighted). The panel at the right shows the account code of the fee (for example 4-02-01-030-6 for Business Plate), and these fields:

  • Type Of Charging: for example Fixed Amount. It is shown for information and cannot be changed here.
  • The amount, for example Fix Amount of 50.00, with an edit icon and a trash icon.

6.6 Review a request

This part follows a request after the Permit Evaluator has sent the assessment for approval (Section 4.10). The request is then in the Pending list of this office.

On the Dashboard, the Pending list shows the request with the business name and its category. Use Search, Request Type and Year to narrow the list. Click the request (highlighted).

Figure 6.42. Click the request in the Pending list (highlighted) to open it.
