Section 11

Offices: Reviewing Requests

After a request is assessed, the offices that are involved review it. All the offices use the same screens, so this section describes them once, using the Engineering office as the example, and then lists what is different for each office.

The figures show the Engineering account, whose name, eBPLS Engineering, is highlighted in red in the first figure. The final approver, the Municipal Treasurer's Office (Bplo in the system), is covered in Section 12.

11.1 The offices

These offices review a request:

  1. Assessor (Municipal Assessor's Office).
  2. Engineering (Municipal Engineering Office).
  3. Fire (Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)).
  4. Peso (Public Employment Service Office).
  5. Mao (Municipal Agriculture Office).
  6. Menro (Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office).
  7. Mpdo (Municipal Planning and Development Office).
  8. Rhu (Rural Health Unit).
  9. Barangay (the barangay where the business is located).
  10. Bplo (the Municipal Treasurer's Office, account eBPLS Assessment): the final approver. It has its own section, Section 12.

The Permit Evaluator, who assesses the request first, is covered in Section 10. The applicant sees each office in the status list of the request (Section 5.8).

11.2 Sign in and open the dashboard

Sign in with the account of the office, as described in Section 2.2. The dashboard of the office opens. For the Engineering office its title is Engineering Administrator Dashboard.

Figure 11.1. The dashboard of the Engineering office, with the account highlighted in red. The business names of other applicants are hidden.

The dashboard has three lists of requests. The number beside each name shows how many requests it holds.

  • Pending: requests waiting for the office to act. Use Search, Request Type and Year to find a request.
  • Disapproved: requests the office has disapproved.
  • Approved: requests the office has approved.

The panel on the right, Request With Attachment, lists requests that have attachments, with the Business, Description, Status and Remarks. It says No record found. when there are none.

The sidebar has Dashboard, and under Reports, Request For Clearances, Holdings and Requirements, and the Applications links (Mail, Chat and Calendar).

11.3 Open a request

In the Pending list, click the business of the request (highlighted).

Figure 11.2. Click the business in the Pending list (highlighted) to open its request.

The Business Information panel opens on the right. Use the Make Inquiry link at its top right to send a message to the applicant (see Section 11.7).

Figure 11.3. The Business Information panel of the request.
  • Assessment Request No., Assessment Code and Request Date: the numbers and the date of the request.
  • Transaction Type, Payment Type and Business Year: for example New Business, Annually and 2026.
  • Gross Income: the gross income entered for the request.
  • Business Type: for example Sole Proprietor.
  • Business Name, Franchise Name, Business Activity and Business Category: what the business is.
  • Business Contact, Business Email, Business Plate No. and Business Address: how to reach the business and where it is.
  • Authorized Representative: the person who transacts for the business.
  • No. Of Storey, Total Floor Area and No. Of Employees: the size of the business.

11.5 Approve or disapprove the request

At the bottom of the request, the Actions card shows the Date of the last action, and the Current Status, for example PENDING. It has five buttons:

  • Pending: puts the request back to pending. It is greyed out while the request is already pending.
  • Disapproved: disapproves the request. Write your remarks.
  • Approved: approves the request. Write your remarks.
  • Location: shows the location of the business.
  • Files: shows the files uploaded by the offices for the request.
Figure 11.15. The Actions card. Click Disapproved or Approved (highlighted) to give your decision.

To approve a request

  1. Click Approved in the Actions card. The Approved Request window opens.
  2. Type your Remarks in the box. The remarks are required, and the applicant will read them. In the example, the remarks are Cleared.
  3. Click Yes (highlighted) to approve, or No to close the window without approving.
Figure 11.16. The Approved Request window. Type the remarks and click Yes (highlighted).

A green message, Request Approved Successful, appears at the lower left. The request leaves the Pending list, and the numbers beside the lists change. In the example, Pending goes down by one and Approved goes up to 1.

Figure 11.17. The dashboard after approving. The message Request Approved Successful appears at the lower left. The business names of other applicants are hidden.

Open the Approved list to find the request. The approved request is listed there (highlighted).

Figure 11.18. The Approved list with the request that was approved. The business names of other applicants are hidden.

The applicant sees your decision and your remarks in the status list of the request: a green dot for approved. Clicking the arrow next to the office shows the remarks (Figure 11.19 and Figure 11.20). The offices that have not acted yet keep a red dot.

Figure 11.19. The status list on the applicant's side. Engineering has a green dot and its remarks.
Figure 11.20. A closer view of the status list.

To disapprove a request

  1. Click Disapproved in the Actions card. The Disapproved Request window opens.
  2. Type your Remarks, which are required. Say what is wrong, so the applicant knows what to correct. In the example, the remarks are Discrepancy Found.
  3. Click Yes (highlighted) to confirm, or No to close the window.
Figure 11.21. The Disapproved Request window. Type the remarks and click Yes (highlighted).

A green message appears at the lower left. The message reads Request Approved Successful even when you disapprove a request. This is the wording of the system. The request leaves the Pending list and the number beside Disapproved goes up.

Figure 11.22. The dashboard after disapproving. The business names of other applicants are hidden.

Open the Disapproved list to find the request (highlighted).

Figure 11.23. The Disapproved list with the request that was disapproved.

The applicant sees an orange dot and your remarks in the status list of the request (Figure 11.24).

Figure 11.24. The status list on the applicant's side. Engineering has an orange dot and its remarks.

To change a decision

A decision is not final. Open the request from the Approved list or the Disapproved list. In the Actions card, the Current Status, the Date and your Remarks show the last decision, and the button of the current decision is greyed out. The other buttons stay available:

  • Pending: puts the request back to pending, as if the office had not acted yet. The request returns to the Pending list.
  • Disapproved: changes an approved request to disapproved.
  • Approved: changes a disapproved request to approved.

For an approved request, Pending and Disapproved are available (highlighted), and Approved is greyed out:

Figure 11.25. An approved request. Click Pending or Disapproved (highlighted) to change the decision.

For a disapproved request, Pending and Approved are available (highlighted), and Disapproved is greyed out:

Figure 11.26. A disapproved request. Click Pending or Approved (highlighted) to change the decision.

Clicking one of the buttons opens the same window, where you write your remarks and confirm. The new decision replaces the old one, and the applicant sees the new dot and the new remarks.

11.6 When an office can act

  • The request must be assessed. A request appears in an office's Pending list only after the Permit Evaluator has approved its details and the assessment has been created (Section 10.5).
  • The office must have an amount in the assessment. If the assessment has no fee with an amount for the office, the action buttons are hidden and the page says Action buttons are hidden due to Assessment - ... amount not set.
  • First quarter or annual requests. Only annual requests and first-quarter requests appear in the offices' Pending lists. Later quarters do not need approval from the offices.
The system does not check whether all the offices approved before Bplo approves. Following the approval of every office is part of the office procedure.

11.7 Inquiries

An office can send a question to an applicant, and read and answer the applicant's questions, on the Inquiries page.

Open the Inquiries page

Figure 11.27. The Inquiries page of an office. Click Make Inquiry (highlighted) to write a new inquiry.

Make an inquiry

  1. Click Make Inquiry (highlighted in Figure 11.27). The Make Inquiry Office window opens.
  2. Click Select Business and choose the business you want to ask about.
Figure 11.28. The Select Business list.
  1. Type your message in Enter message inquiry.
  2. Click Send (highlighted), or Cancel to close the window without sending.
Figure 11.29. The Make Inquiry Office window, filled in. Click Send (highlighted) to send the inquiry.

A green message, Inquiry successfully sent., appears at the lower left.

Figure 11.30. The Inquiries page after the inquiry is sent.

Read and answer an inquiry

Click an inquiry in the List Of Inquiries to open it. The conversation opens on the right. It shows the business the inquiry is about and the date, and the messages in the conversation. Your messages are in a blue box. The reply of the applicant appears in the same conversation.

Figure 11.31. An inquiry opened. The conversation shows the message sent to the applicant.

To answer, type your message in the Message box at the bottom of the conversation and click the send icon at its right. It works as the applicant's chat described in Section 9.3.

11.8 Request For Clearances

The Request For Clearances page, under Reports in the sidebar, is a report of the requests that came to your office. Use it to look back at requests by year, status and date.

The Request Parameters are:

  • Year: choose a year, or all.
  • Status: choose a status, or all.
  • Date From and Date To: the period to show. The page opens with the current month.

Click Search (highlighted) to show the list.

Figure 11.32. The Request For Clearances page. Choose the parameters and click Search (highlighted).

The list shows List of all Company and the period you chose. Use Search Business to find a business. The two icons at the right of the search box download the list:

  • The PDF icon downloads the list as a PDF file.
  • The document icon downloads the list as an Excel (.xlsx) file.
Figure 11.33. The search box and the two download icons (highlighted): download as PDF and download as .xlsx.

The columns are:

  • Id: the number of the row.
  • Business Name: the name of the business.
  • Transaction Date and Transaction Year: when and for which year the request was made.
  • Payment Mode and Payment Quarter: how the applicant pays and, for a quarterly request, the quarter.
  • Transaction Type: for example New Business.
  • Business Type, Business Activity and Business Category: what the business is.
  • Remarks: the decision of your office, for example Approved.
  • Amount: the amount of the request.

11.10 Requirements

The Requirements page, under Reports in the sidebar, is the List Of Requirments of your office. These are the requirements your office asks applicants to send. The list is on the left and the New Requirements form is on the right.

Figure 11.37. The List Of Requirements. The pencil icon (highlighted) changes a requirement.

The Requirements List has a search box (Search bus type, role, desc.), and these columns:

  • Id: the number of the requirement.
  • Office: your office.
  • Business Type: the business type the requirement applies to, when one is set.
  • Requirements: the name of the requirement.
  • Attachments: a form or file attached to the requirement. It says No attachment. when there is none.
  • Action: the pencil icon changes the requirement, and the trash icon removes it.

To add a requirement

  1. In the New Requirements form, type the name of the requirement in Requirement.
  2. Choose Is Required: true if the applicant must send it.
  3. Choose Is Default, Yes or No.
  4. Click Select Business Type and choose the business type the requirement is for from the list. You can also type to narrow the list.
  5. If your office has a form for this requirement, click Choose Files under Attachments to attach it. The applicant can download it.
  6. Tick the kinds of request the requirement is for: New, Renewal, Transfer and Closure.
  7. Click Save (highlighted), or Cancel to clear the form.
Figure 11.38. The New Requirements form filled in. Click Save (highlighted).
Figure 11.39. The Select Business Type list shows the business types.

A green message, Requirements successfully added., appears at the lower left, and the new requirement is added to the list.

Figure 11.40. The list after the requirement is added.

To change a requirement

  1. Click the pencil icon in the Action column of the requirement (highlighted in Figure 11.37). The Edit Requirements window opens with the details of the requirement.
  2. Change what you need: the Requirement, Is Required, Is Default, the business type, the attachment, and the kinds of request (New, Renewal, Transfer, Closure).
  3. Click Save (highlighted), or Cancel to close the window without changing it.
Figure 11.41. The Edit Requirements window. Click Save (highlighted) to keep your changes.

To remove a requirement

Click the trash icon in the Action column of the requirement. The window asks Are you sure to remove this requirement? Click Yes (highlighted) to remove it, or No to keep it.

Figure 11.42. The confirmation window for removing a requirement.
Changes to the requirements of your office affect the requirements the applicants see for their requests.
